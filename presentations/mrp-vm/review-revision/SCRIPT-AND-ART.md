# MRP-VM — the research result that did not become a practice

Status: complete proposal for root review; no voice or live edits.

Story spine: A research team cannot reproduce the quality of a theory comparison or explain what changed; the book proposes preserving a bounded procedure and its tests in a versioned capability, then separating candidate improvements from activation. Reader gain is knowing what must be retained and evaluated to turn an answer into a maintained practice.

## Complete proposed speech

### MRP-VM
1. MRP-VM asks why one excellent research answer may disappear on the next request.
2. Its opening thought experiment compares a brilliant theory assessment with a shallow repeat.
3. Readers learn to preserve the procedure, sources, tests, and authority behind recurring work.
4. The proposed architecture gives those responsibilities a versioned home around powerful models.
5. An improvement remains a candidate until evaluation and publication authorize a new active version.

### An answer is not a capability
1. The first comparison identifies decisive disagreements between two theories and suggests an experiment.
2. A week later, a similar request produces a weaker comparison without an explainable cause.
3. An Agentic Knowledge Unit gives the procedure, sources, tests, and interpreter a maintained identity.
4. That structure makes changes inspectable when another request arrives under a recorded configuration.
5. Repeatability still requires varied tests, including cases where that procedure should stay inactive.

### A candidate stays a candidate
1. Operational feedback can motivate a candidate revision without changing the active procedure immediately.
2. The proposed process compares candidates against baselines and checks for regressions.
3. Proposal, evaluation, approval, and activation remain separate responsibilities.
4. Published versions retain a coherent identity and a target for rollback.
5. The book uses these distinctions to connect improvement with control over consequential change.

### Read the maintained practice
1. Start with the gap between a good answer and a repeatable organizational practice.
2. Then follow the architecture through explicit work, qualification, trace, and controlled improvement.
3. Follow Chapter Seven's capability units into Chapters Eleven and Twelve's evaluation and publication controls.
4. Those chapters explain which change becomes active, what evidence supports it, and how rollback works.
5. Read MRP-VM to evaluate how model capability might become maintained institutional capability.
