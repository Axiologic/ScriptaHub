1. The Society That Selects Us examines a paradox inside modern success.
2. A society can reward education while making family life harder to sustain.
3. This scientific and philosophical essay follows that tension through fertility, personality, and institutions.
4. It separates genetic change from cultural transmission, social filtering, and demographic shifts.
5. That separation helps readers examine selection without mistaking every social change for biological destiny.
