# The Ultimate Sense of Life — proposed replacement

A serious comedy brings grand explanations back to lived obligations, uses its actual cosmic-support joke to separate questions, makes the proposed FIRE framework intelligible through one roof repair, then tests that meaning against exclusion and labor.

Proposal only; current production and public film retained.

## A serious comedy

1. The Ultimate Sense of Life asks why cosmic answers must matter to ordinary lives.
2. Its serious comedy brings religions, laboratories, philosophers, and working people into one argument.
3. The opening wager is that life makes existence capable of caring about existence.
4. Then the book asks whether that care survives contact with actual obligations.

Art: An earnest thinker studies an impressively long rolled argument while another adult catches drops from a leak in a bucket beside them. The thinker finally looks up from the scroll toward the affected person. The scroll is a conceptual representation of grand explanation, not a fabricated source quotation. Short meaningful labels only; no giant secondary slogan. The mismatch is warm comedy, not contempt for philosophy, poverty or belief.

Emotion: Curiosity through gentle incongruity, then attention to a real beneficiary.

## A complaint to existence

1. The book imagines humanity complaining that existence arrived without a user manual.
2. Cosmic support offers another restart, although previous attempts caused severe data loss.
3. Behind the joke lies a distinction between cosmic purpose and lived meaning.
4. Psychology can study how life feels meaningful without proving a universal intention.
5. That distinction lets the book compare religious and philosophical answers without collapsing them.

Art: Enact the actual literary support interlude: a tired caller has an empty manual sleeve; a plainly theatrical cosmic support desk suggests a restart. An old record of civilization visibly loses pages rather than exploding a planet. The caller points to the missing pages, and the proposed restart remains pending. The support conversation is visibly imaginary, not a scientific cosmology or portrayal of any religion. End on two actual questions on separate pages—How does life feel meaningful? and Was life intended?—with no implied experimental answer to the second.

Emotion: Comic release, then a quiet distinction that makes the larger debate intelligible.

## Meaning in the repair

1. The roof repairer needs no theory explaining why the cosmos intended timber.
2. A storm approaches, someone needs shelter, and skilled work can make a difference.
3. The proposed FIRE model names four parts of such lived meaning.
4. Finitude gives time stakes, while integration connects action with commitments we understand.
5. Relation concerns who or what matters, while enactment turns that value into practice.
6. FIRE is a philosophical synthesis and research proposal, not an established psychological scale.

Art: Close cutaway of the same leaking roof: storm approaches in the relevant exterior area; the repairer chooses the needed tile, fits it, and the interior bucket stops receiving drops. The resident can set the bucket aside. Limited time, an understood commitment, a beneficiary and skilled action are shown through one situation, never four noun cards or an arithmetic formula. Small labels may appear beside the meaningful object one at a time. This is the source’s conceptual roof example, not a measured study or instruction for roof work.

Emotion: Recognition becomes explanatory payoff; relief comes from an intelligible action rather than a cosmic answer.

## Whose roof gets repaired

1. But feeling purposeful does not automatically make an action good.
2. The book asks whose roof gets repaired and who is left outside.
3. Its workers challenge grand answers that praise dignity while underpaying actual labor.
4. Begin with Those Who Need the Question, then meet the fictional ordinary witnesses.
5. Read it to test your answers against care, freedom, and the lives they affect.

Art: Widen the view: one household remains dry while a neighboring adult stands beneath a still-leaking eave; a closed entrance makes selective protection visible. The repairer notices the excluded person and pauses to reconsider, without inventing a resolved reform or identifying an ethnic/religious group. On the labor line, the worker returns an inadequate offered payment rather than accepting praise as compensation. End with two readable real reading labels: Those Who Need the Question and Eight Ordinary Cosmologies on a well-proportioned open volume. Do not portray the fictional witnesses as photographed research participants.

Emotion: Comfort is challenged by exclusion and respect, then an earned reading invitation.
