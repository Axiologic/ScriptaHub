# Cones of Meaning — source-grounded replacement proposal

Status: root-approved script and original artwork installed after all-theme silent QA; measured duration162866ms.

Purpose: English narrated book introduction, 120–180 seconds. One sentence per clip, 1.25–1.4 seconds of actual silence between sentences, 2 seconds at scene changes. Final timing will use measured Piper speech. No audible background preview.

Story spine: a hospital can optimize an accurate waiting-time measure while obscuring unfinished care; this book uses such mismatches to examine how institutions and AI make some questions feel natural while leaving other purposes outside.

The reading gain is concrete: examine the purpose and excluded consequences inside a persuasive answer while preserving evidence and the need to decide. The film does not advertise a universal theory or reduce the source to another AI-safety lecture.

## Complete spoken draft

### 1. Cones of Meaning

1. Cones of Meaning asks how careful reasoning can serve the wrong question.
2. Its hospital example shows a waiting-time target crowding out the purpose of care.
3. The same problem becomes urgent when AI generates persuasive explanations from competing perspectives.
4. You learn to question what a coherent answer makes visible and leaves outside.

### 2. The number improves

1. The hospital measures waiting time because delay is a real problem.
2. Once that measure becomes the target, difficult cases can be reclassified.
3. Consultations can also become shorter, making the reported process look more efficient.
4. Care that escapes the number may then appear to be wasted time.
5. The book asks whether the measure still represents what the institution should protect.

### 3. What enters the question

1. Through an efficiency frame, the queue's speed becomes the main problem.
2. Through a patient's experience, an unfinished conversation may matter more.
3. The book calls such selective orientations cones of meaning.
4. Their geometry is a metaphor for selection, not a literal model anatomy.

### 4. Coherence follows a direction

1. The book began partly with an AI extending the author's incompletely stated philosophy.
2. That experience raises a practical question about how prompts orient apparently coherent reasoning.
3. Ask for efficiency, and a hospital proposal may foreground shorter consultations.
4. Ask about care, and unfinished conversations may become central instead.
5. The model's coherence does not establish which purpose should govern the decision.

### 5. Choose the question

1. The chapters trace ideas into institutions, then examine how language models reproduce orientations.
2. Meta-rationality examines the frame while keeping evidence and reasoning answerable to criticism.
3. Outfinitism adds finite time, resources, and responsibility for actually deciding.
4. Start with The Age of Cheap Coherence and its hospital example.
5. Read Cones of Meaning to question the purpose hidden inside a convincing answer.
