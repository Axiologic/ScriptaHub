# The Makers of Reality

## After the last price

The Makers of Reality explores a world where money no longer organizes access.

Its novel begins with needed goods immobilized by incompatible valid instructions.

Later abundance changes the problem without removing decisions about allocation.

The novel examines how power changes when prices disappear.

The book follows material needs into the less visible rules governing their satisfaction.

Abundance supplies possibilities while leaving disagreement about their consequences intact.

## Instructions without movement

The opening shipment contains filters that hospitals need to receive.

Its governing instructions can each be valid while remaining impossible to satisfy together.

The drone obeys them all by staying where it is.

That impasse makes coordination a visible problem before the economic order changes.

Money is part of the story, but it is not its only organizing device.

The novel asks what happens when access and authority must be described differently.

## A workshop without prices

Later, ordinary goods can be obtained without the familiar act of purchasing them.

Their availability does not erase water, effort, attention, or conflicting priorities.

Allocation makes those consequences objects of interpretation and public argument.

The story follows people who describe needs and others who question the descriptions.

That shifts political pressure toward what becomes visible enough to count.

The reading experience joins an economic transformation to everyday negotiations over a shared world.

## Follow what replaces the price

Start with The Last Price and its motionless, urgently needed shipment.

Then compare the later city where buying no longer organizes ordinary access.

The contrast explains why this is a novel about power as well as abundance.

Its appeal is the concrete life of choices that an economic slogan leaves unresolved.

Readers can follow how a need becomes a claim on shared capacity.

The book makes the disappearance of prices the beginning of an inquiry.
