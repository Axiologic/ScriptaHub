# S*LM — source example revision proposal

Status: complete proposed20-line script and art plan; awaiting root editorial review before voice or live film changes.

Spine: One supplier-restriction request requires interpretation, policy retrieval, exact exposure calculation and a draft-only constraint; allocating these distinct obligations explains the book's research programme and why smaller is not automatically better.

Seven sentence edits preserve thirteen existing recordings. All later evidence and economic qualifications remain. The scenario is the book's conceptual enterprise request, not a live policy/compliance recommendation or demonstration of an implemented product.

## Complete proposed speech

### Scene 1
1. S*LM asks which parts of an agent's work actually require a general language model.
2. The star includes small, specialized, and symbolic components, without treating them as interchangeable.
3. Its supplier example separates understanding a restriction from calculating exposure and authorizing communication.
4. The book asks how much generality each role actually needs.
5. Readers can evaluate a research programme about allocation, rather than another promise of universal replacement.

### Scene 2
1. A request asks for affected suppliers and exposure, while explicitly forbidding outgoing messages.
2. Interpreting the restriction requires resolving ambiguity and retrieving the authoritative policy version.
3. A tested calculation uses structured supplier records instead of inventing arithmetic in prose.
4. The drafting component receives approved results but no authority to send them.
5. Readers can follow each obligation into the contracts governing different components.

### Scene 3
1. Repeated work may justify a compact policy, deterministic procedure, or specialized model.
2. That possibility depends on maintenance, changing tasks, verification, and end-to-end performance.
3. A cheaper component is not a saving if the surrounding workflow becomes unreliable.
4. The book states hypotheses and conditions that could weaken its proposed approach.
5. It keeps access to general models and human judgment where narrower components remain insufficient.

### Scene 4
1. Begin with the taxonomy separating size, specialization, and symbolic structure.
2. Then follow the language stack from interpretation through action, verification, and recovery.
3. The research programme asks how role requirements can guide choices across different workflows.
4. S*LM offers a way to evaluate heterogeneous systems without assuming specialization always wins.
5. Read it to decide where general intelligence is needed, and where a narrower obligation is clearer.

## Original art plan

Palette and staging: warm rust procurement workspace, ink-blue structured records, yellow-orange exact calculation, mint known procedures. Distinguish the human request from software obligations; do not show a row of human-role avatars pretending each component is a person. No brain icons, model-size bar charts or generic gears.

1. A procurement analyst holds the same request beside a visible unsent draft. Its two linked clauses are immediately legible: affected suppliers/exposure, and do not send. A restriction reference remains ambiguous until resolved. The request governs the work; a large general model is neither declared obsolete nor given all authority.
2. Close enacted progression across that one request: resolve policy reference, bind supplier identifiers to records, calculate exposure using a versioned procedure, draft a change while the communication control remains unavailable. Keep the governing prohibition visible across the transformation. Record rows and procedure fields are schematic and carry no fabricated supplier names or monetary values. Preserve the separation between interpreted meaning and a syntactically valid artifact.
3. Repeated instances of the same bounded operation enter a maintained procedure; an unfamiliar restriction remains at a clarification/escalation branch. A return-for-repair path has a visible cost in extra steps. This is an illustrative lifecycle, not measured savings or an invented benchmark. The no-send gate persists into the final draft.
4. Pull back to the analyst comparing the interpreted request with the proposed draft and retained evidence. An open copy of S*LM connects taxonomy, action contracts and the research hypotheses; the reader gain is choosing an appropriate owner for each obligation. No eventual message is sent and no approval is inferred.
