# Borrowed Credibility — complete proposed revision

Status: source-reviewed root draft, awaiting independent review before voice or public changes.
Target: measured 2–3 minutes, English local Piper, one sentence per clip, ordinary pauses 1.45 seconds and scene transitions 2 seconds; no speed-up.

## Why → how → what

Why: scientific reputation is necessary, but can silently answer a different question from whether a particular claim is supported.
How: follow the book's expressly composite manuscript through changed pedigree, direct inspection and local correction.
What: introduce its institutional proposal to fund and credit verification, with contestable AI assistance, then invite readers to follow the complete incentive argument.
This is a book introduction, not a claim that the reforms already work or an attack on expertise.

## Full spoken script

### Scene 1 — The manuscript stays unchanged
1. Borrowed Credibility asks when a respected name starts doing the work of evidence.
2. This book explains scientific trust through institutions that must ration scarce attention.
3. Its opening thought experiment gives the same manuscript two different institutional pedigrees.
4. The words stay unchanged, but a famous laboratory can alter expectations.
5. Readers follow that gap without assuming either experts or outsiders are automatically right.

### Scene 2 — Inspect the work
1. The recurring researcher, Elena Varga, is a composite created for this institutional journey.
2. Her manuscript lets the book separate attention, competence, evidence, and access to resources.
3. A laboratory's reputation can reasonably tell a reviewer where to begin looking.
4. It cannot replace checking the particular result once its evidence becomes available.
5. The proposed alternative connects claims with methods, data, code, and independent checks.
6. Those connections reveal what supports a claim, while leaving judgment open to challenge.

### Scene 3 — Keep the useful parts
1. A paper can contain a reliable measurement and a much weaker interpretation.
2. Imagine a reviewer questioning that interpretation while retaining the underlying measurement.
3. The book's proposed infrastructure would make such distinctions easier to inspect and update.
4. It also asks who receives time, money, and credit for doing this work.
5. Better records alone cannot repair incentives that reward production while neglecting correction.

### Scene 4 — Follow the cost of checking
1. Later chapters examine governance, status-sensitive reviews, and AI assistance with verification.
2. Automated mappings remain contestable, because another opaque authority could reproduce the original problem.
3. Begin with the composite manuscript, then follow the chapters on incentives and correction.
4. Read to understand how scientific trust could become more specific and easier to revise.
5. The book offers institutional proposals to examine, rather than a guarantee against error.

## Original visual direction

Ink-blue, burnished copper, warm grey paper and clear turquoise measurement marks; large calm negative space. One bold Red Hat Display heading per scene, captions below all physical action. No prestige trophy, halo, scales, abstract network or person beside three icons. Metaphors are actual research objects and review work; no book-shaped institution.

Elena is the source's composite researcher, not a portrait of a real individual. Cast consistently as a dark-haired adult in a copper cardigan; appearance is illustrative. The reviewer is a distinct older adult in ink-blue with close-cropped grey hair. Keep body, hand and supported object geometry coherent. Faces register expectation, inspection, uncertainty and settled attention; do not make reputationally favoured researchers villains.

### Scene 1: overhead submission desk

A large manuscript lies on a real desktop; its top margin, three small turquoise data marks and copper page tab form a persistent identity. Two plain detachable cover slips appear successively: “Identity withheld”, then “Known laboratory”. A hand lifts only the cover slip. The body text/data/page tab remain exactly fixed. A reviewer’s magnifying lens moves toward the same result after the famous affiliation is revealed, with a small pause of expectation, not an invented numerical acceptance score. Retain explicit narrated thought-experiment status. No real institution logo, fake scientist quote or measured prestige effect.

Shift from overhead document to close face/lens at line4 so the meaning is human expectation, not a magical changing scientific result. Keep the original sheet visible in both views. The full manuscript title is not duplicated in the art; a tiny “Manuscript” working label is enough.
Emotional arc: curiosity → recognition → unsettled but fair-minded attention.
Checks: unchanged page/data node identity through slip exchange; fingers contact actual slip; lens carries no verdict and does not magnify text into the caption area.

### Scene 2: seated review bench with a physical source insert

Side view: same manuscript between Elena and reviewer, on a broad supported tabletop. Opening line identifies Elena as a composite in narration. The previous affiliation cover is set aside, visible but peripheral. A foldout supporting record opens adjacent to the manuscript. Show only two focal areas at once: a simple three-mark measurement record and its referenced manuscript sentence. A finger traces from the relevant measurement to its corresponding statement; an actual thin pointer ends at the marks, not at the author's name.

Do not draw five “methods/data/code/checks” cards. Those are narrated as the scope of the proposal, while one physically inspectable relationship demonstrates it. At line5 open a narrow source margin labelled “Supporting record”; line6 the reviewer writes a small question mark on a separate review note rather than stamping approved. No claim that seeing three marks validates real research. These marks are expressly schematic illustration, with no invented units, effect size or experiment.
Emotional arc: orientation → focused examination → productive uncertainty.
Checks: source insert and manuscript remain distinct; finger/pen reach natural; note is supported; affiliation remains available for appropriate context rather than erased from the world.

### Scene 3: close annotation, then the work required

Close-up of the same manuscript and foldout. Two visible short section labels: “Measurement” beside three persistent marks, and “Interpretation” beside a short text stroke. The narrator explicitly introduces an imagined review. A pen brackets the interpretation and places “Needs review” beside it. Do not cross out the data, erase authorship, show the reviewer as proven correct, or make the whole paper fail. The measurement is retained in this illustration, not independently certified by the film.

At line3 the lens pulls back from the margin to reveal the reviewer seated at the desk doing the work. At line4 their clock advances modestly while a time sheet receives one entry labelled “Verification”; this represents labour that needs funding, not guaranteed paid reform. Beside it an ordinary empty budget field labelled “Time allocation” remains unresolved. End on the reviewer holding the question for discussion, not applauding a completed solution.
Emotional arc: concern → precise distinction → recognition of maintenance work.
Checks: the same three measurement marks stay unchanged through the annotation; bracket points only at interpretation; pen stays joined to hand; time sheet is held or desk-supported, labels stay clear.

### Scene 4: reader's working copy

An ordinary prospective reader sits with a supported open book and the manuscript facsimile tucked beside it. This is the book itself, not a model of the research system. Show one navigation detail at a time: “The Paper From Nowhere”, then “Paying for correction”. A pencil bookmark moves from the first page to the later heading as the narrator recommends the route, leaving both the purpose and breadth of the book clear.

For AI assistance mention, a small screen at the edge shows an extracted reference connection with a visible “Check mapping” note; the reader compares it to the source passage, rather than accepting a score. Remove this insert before the reading invitation. Close with hands supporting the book and a thoughtful look back at the unchanged manuscript; avoid triumph or a magical universal evidence graph.
Emotional arc: wider curiosity → caution → practical reading invitation.
Checks: screen proposal remains explicitly pending; book rests on lap/table, fingers touch pages and pencil; no competing tiny labels; end titles read comfortably at phone scale.
