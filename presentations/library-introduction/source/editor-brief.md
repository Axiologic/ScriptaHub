# Editorial purpose supplied by the project owner

The virtual library enables niche books that conventional human publishing may
not support economically. Books provide sustained depth on complicated subjects.
Experts contribute questions, structure and corrections; AI develops books and
new editions from that direction. Readers reuse the human thinking invested in
a well-designed book, rather than reconstructing the exploration through repeated
agent conversations. For a well-scoped, well-written book, that can offer greater
depth and reduce repeated computation, cost and energy. No measured saving or
universal superiority is claimed. The same opportunity applies to science fiction
and other literary forms whose small audiences still matter culturally.

Lead discovery through Ask AI Librarian. Do not narrate email delivery; the public
presentation should explain the contribution intent independently of a temporary
transport. Account-based contributions are planned, not claimed as available here.
