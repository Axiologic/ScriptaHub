# OpenDSU — targeted source-grounded revision proposal

Status: complete proposed script and art plan; awaiting root editorial review before voice or canonical edits.

Spine: A research dataset leaves its originating application; a copied payload loses surrounding history and policy, a DSU keeps reconstructable content related to authority and continuity, but an intact history cannot settle a contested measurement.

Reading gain: Learn which relationships to require when sharing a dataset, and distinguish verification of an object from independent evidence for its scientific claims.

Six sentence edits preserve fourteen recorded clips. The original conceptual-framework scope stays explicit; no current API or product capability claim is added.

## Complete proposed speech

### Scene 1
1. OpenDSU asks what a research dataset must preserve when it leaves its original application.
2. The book explains a conceptual framework, rather than documenting one frozen software release.
3. A receiving team needs the data, its history, and authority for the intended use.
4. The framework binds those relationships while keeping confidential content separate from public continuity evidence.
5. Readers gain distinctions for evaluating digital trust without asking one mechanism to guarantee everything.

### Scene 2
1. An exported dataset can retain its values while losing transformation history or permitted-use policy.
2. A Data Sharing Unit instead gives related information an accountable logical boundary.
3. Protected bricks and a protected map support reconstruction of the object's contents.
4. Compact anchoring evidence records continuity without placing the confidential contents on a shared ledger.
5. The book explains why those relationships matter beyond any particular storage implementation.

### Scene 3
1. Reading, modification, and verification need not expose the same powers or information.
2. The book distinguishes capability, provenance, validation, and the context where authority is exercised.
3. Each mechanism answers a limited question about the digital object.
4. The preserved dataset might still contain a false measurement that requires independent investigation.
5. That separation lets readers ask which evidence is sufficient for their intended decision.

### Scene 4
1. Begin with the verification gap before entering the technical vocabulary.
2. Follow the chapters on bounded objects, reconstruction, authority, and history as one connected design.
3. The evaluation chapter explains what each mechanism leaves unresolved.
4. OpenDSU is useful as a conceptual guide to controllable and verifiable information exchange.
5. Read it to judge which relationships your own digital objects need to preserve.

## Original art plan

Palette: cobalt laboratory screens, pale porcelain, coral transfer markers, copper continuity marks and muted violet for protected storage. Research collaborators have distinct receiving and observing roles. The physical staging is a schematic research exchange, not a claim that DSUs pack all content into one archive.

1. A shared research record moves between two large workstation displays across an institutional threshold. The same plotted measurement survives in the receiving view while transformation notes and permitted-use context remain on the sender side. A receiving researcher examines those missing relationships. The first thirty seconds ties this actual information gap to the book's conceptual framework.
2. A close reconstruction view follows the same dataset: two separate opaque storage areas supply protected pieces through a protected structural map into an authorized receiving screen. A compact version-history ribbon remains separate from the confidential view. Matching markers express binding, not a single physical container; meaningful staged movement shows assembly rather than three floating icons.
3. Two people examine different surfaces of that same object. One has a content-reading view; another sees only compact continuity evidence. Reading carries no edit handle. Then a particular measurement is questioned while the intact history remains unchanged; independent scientific investigation sits outside cryptographic verification. No actual empirical finding or sensor failure is invented.
4. Return to the receiver with a bounded report separating content integrity, authority, history and the still-disputed measurement. The book's path from verification gap to reconstruction and evaluation limits sits in an open reading copy, not a generic trust badge. No all-purpose valid stamp, automatic truth, or declared guarantee.
