# Relevance — source-specific context revision

Status: full proposed script for root review before voice; four edits preserve sixteen recordings.

Spine: The same rare-failure detection method can be peripheral in a theory group, needed by clinical safety reviewers and urgent to a regulator after an incident; relevance is an argued relation to a decision, not a quality property inside the artifact.

Reader gain: Learn to state for whom, for which purpose and at what time a work matters, without equating relevance with popularity or suppressing unfamiliar future work.

## Complete proposed speech

### Scene 1
1. Relevance asks what deserves attention when creating competent work becomes inexpensive.
2. It separates relevance from quality, novelty, popularity, and compliance with a brief.
3. The same artifact can matter differently as its audience, purpose, or circumstances change.
4. The book develops that argument across research, literature, film, design, and visual art.
5. Readers gain a framework for defending judgments while preserving space for unfamiliar work.

### Scene 2
1. One example follows a method for detecting a rare failure in medical AI.
2. For a theoretical research group, that method may remain peripheral.
3. A clinical safety team may need it immediately.
4. A public incident could later make the unchanged method urgent for a regulator.
5. This distinction connects its scientific examples with its discussion of culture and design.

### Scene 3
1. A relevance dossier begins with the decision, affected people, values, constraints, and time horizon.
2. It connects specific claims to evidence, uncertainty, and possible counterarguments.
3. Different critics may assess feasibility, novelty, user value, or cultural contribution.
4. Their disagreement is useful information, not noise that a final score must erase.
5. The book also warns that multiple evaluators can share the same mistakes.

### Scene 4
1. A system trained on earlier success can overlook work whose value is not yet recognized.
2. The book therefore preserves a role for exploration and difficult-to-classify contributions.
3. Its chapters ask how institutions can explain selection without fully optimizing inherited preferences.
4. Relevance is useful when competent output has become abundant and attention remains limited.
5. Read it to examine both the reasons for choosing work and the costs of overlooking it.

## Original art plan

Identity: magenta-corner method booklet, turquoise clinical-review spaces, indigo theory studio, copper regulatory records; later a warm coral/lavender small screening room. The film changes places and scale, not merely the color of the same card row.

1. The same method booklet sits between two different tasks in a shared research reading room. A theorist continues a foundational problem while a clinical safety reviewer reaches for the rare-failure method. There is no quality-star score, popularity badge or implied universal superiority. Their attention follows different needs.
2. The source method passes through three sequential views within the scene: theory workspace, clinical safety review and regulatory review after a generic incident notice. Preserve the booklet's visible identity exactly. The theory group can leave it in a reference pile; the clinical team brings it into the active review; the later incident makes the regulator reopen it. No diagnosis, harm details, named failure or quantitative model performance is invented. The incident is a conditional conceptual notice, not a real news report.
3. Close view of the resulting review record retains the actor, decision and time along with the same method. Two reviewers attach different questions about usefulness and evidence scope. They do not vote a universal relevance score into existence. A common cited source remains visibly shared when the warning about correlated errors arrives.
4. The book's cross-domain argument appears in a small screening room: a curator gives an unfamiliar work a limited exploration slot beside familiar programmes. That is an opportunity to examine it, not proof of artistic value or a demonstrated audience success. The final reading route returns to research, culture and the dossier's revisable decisions.
