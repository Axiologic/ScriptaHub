Before Explanation: keeping our stories open to correction Before Explanation: keeping our stories open to correction How distinctions, evidence and explicit limits can make explanation a safer guide to action. The comfort of a finished story Before Explanation begins with the relief a coherent story brings. A product fails, a relationship breaks, or a project misses its deadline, and uncertainty quickly becomes a confident account of what happened. The danger arrives when that provisional account becomes the environment within which every later decision is made. The book defends explanation as indispensable to science and practical life, while asking us to preserve the difference between an event and the representation through which we understand it. Every explanation selects features, scales and questions. A useful model need not retain every detail; it must discard detail appropriately for its purpose. A theory about manufacturers shortening appliance lifetimes does not establish why this particular toaster stopped working. Likewise, biological, psychological and social explanations can address different aspects of one problem. Their coexistence becomes confusing when their scope is left unmarked. Good explanation therefore identifies what it represents, for which purpose and under which conditions. The feeling of understanding is weaker evidence than the ability to specify a mechanism. The book discusses research in which people discovered gaps when asked to explain familiar devices or policies in detail. A list of persuasive reasons can defend a position while leaving its causal structure unexamined. Mechanistic detail creates commitments: what changes, through which pathway, compared with which alternative, and what observation would challenge the account. Scientific explanations gain strength from such opportunities for correction, rather than from immunity to failure. Sincerity provides no automatic guarantee either. People can reconstruct plausible reasons without directly observing every process behind their choices. Generated AI reasoning can likewise omit influences on an answer. These findings do not make every explanation false or every self-report worthless. They make faithfulness an empirical question. A narrative describing what could have happened differs from a record of what information actually entered a decision. When fluent prose becomes cheap, inspected evidence and constraints become more valuable signals of understanding. The choices hidden inside causes Explanations become especially consequential when they quietly change the kind of question being answered. Saying that fear contributed to a lie can explain its cause, make it understandable, or imply an excuse. Those are different claims. Responsibility depends on capacities, knowledge, available alternatives and applicable standards; it is neither untouched by circumstances nor dissolved by discovering causes. Separating cause, constraint, agency, norm and response allows institutional reform and individual accountability to remain possible together. The proposed concept of choice-to-necessity laundering identifies a related move: a real constraint is presented as complete, concealing the values used to reject remaining alternatives. A company may prefer reduced quality over lower margins or slower growth, but preference among costly options is different from having no choice. The practical question is which possibilities remain, what each costs, and which commitments exclude them. A constraint narrows a feasible region; compulsion leaves only one option. Sometimes necessity is real, but its extent needs examination. Institutional categories also contain decisions. An admission threshold can create sharply different outcomes for nearly identical measurements. Diagnoses and risk models can help people while shaping how institutions treat them. They may even alter opportunities or surveillance in ways that generate confirming data. This does not prove that useful knowledge is merely disguised power. It adds questions about authorization, contestability, calibration and who bears errors. When actions impose harm, the book recommends dual description: state the operational purpose and the lived consequences. Understanding why an action occurred must not make its consequences disappear. Distinguish before closing the question Distinguishing before explaining means retaining differences long enough for evidence to discriminate. A report that an application is slow becomes useful when it separates startup, database, network and rendering problems. In conflict, describing missed messages preserves more interpretive possibilities than declaring that someone intended rejection. The goal is a record another theory could inherit. Keep original observations, document transformations, version models and distinguish derived labels from source data. Compression remains necessary, but a path back to consequential detail should survive it. Description is not perfectly neutral. Instruments, attention, language and selection already shape what gets recorded. The improvement lies in making the inferential chain visible. The book separates event, trace, description, pattern, explanation, evaluation and action. A claim about employee commitment can otherwise move silently from one measured correlation to an account of motivation and then to a policy. Examining each transition makes uncertainty and alternative transformations easier to inspect. Corrigibility requires accessible layers, not a promise of direct access to unmediated reality. Serious rival explanations then make evidence work harder. Falling performance might reflect motivation, harder tasks, unclear expectations, a failing metric, health or an unusual comparison period. Ask which observations and interventions would separate those possibilities. More stories alone do not provide more evidence, including when AI generates them. Nor can the alternatives we happened to imagine exhaust the hypothesis space. Rival generation is useful when followed by discriminating predictions, explicit assumptions and affordable tests. Keeping alternatives open cannot become permanent indecision. Closure should depend on stakes, reversibility, urgency and the cost of further evidence. A reversible experiment may justify action under substantial uncertainty, while a costly irreversible commitment requires stronger investigation. Confidence and permission to act are separate judgments. Responsible action can disclose uncertainty, monitor harm, retain rollback paths and identify evidence that would trigger revision. The aim is a safety margin between what the explanation supports and what the decision assumes, rather than pretending that uncertainty has vanished. Explanations need maintenance The book calls dependence on an untested provisional story explanation debt. A business that interprets poor sales as an immature market may build hiring and marketing around that account without reconsidering product value. Debt grows through dependency without revalidation, not simply through an idea&#x27;s age. A proposed register records the claim, evidence, confidence, validated domain, decisions depending on it, serious rivals, failure signals, last audit and exit cost. Its suggested debt score is an engineering scaffold to investigate, not an established universal equation. Maintenance begins with an audit of observation, comparison, model, rivals, discriminating evidence, incentives, normative claims and boundaries. Motivational benefits indicate where stronger checking may help; they do not logically refute a claim. Confidence should also face feedback. Record predictions before outcomes where that is appropriate, compare confidence with accuracy, and notice deterioration outside familiar domains. Retrospective and structural explanations may need other tests. Calibration is valuable because it makes uncertainty accountable, rather than turning verbal modesty into another performance of expertise. Organizations can interrupt unsupported commitments with epistemic circuit breakers. An irreversible decision, weak evidence beneath strong confidence, or use outside a validated domain can trigger a shift from advocacy to audit. Separate the decision owner from the explanation reviewer and consider a reversible pilot. Demanding justification after a decision may produce obedience when the audience&#x27;s favored conclusion is known. The timing, audience and incentives of accountability therefore matter. The same principle protects personal relationships: interpretations of another person&#x27;s motives must remain hypotheses that the person can contest, rather than accounts that turn every objection into confirmation. AI increases the urgency of this maintenance. Important answers should carry inspectable evidence, assumptions, alternatives, task-appropriate uncertainty, verified computations or tool traces, boundaries and known failures. Generated prose is a candidate artifact. It becomes useful through checking, not through its apparent access to the machine&#x27;s mind. Organizations that adopt explanations faster than they validate them can accumulate dependence at machine speed. Map where knowledge stops working Outfinitism names the book&#x27;s proposed research direction: treat the boundary of knowledge as part of knowledge itself. It begins with finite agents, evidence, attention and computation, while allowing an open-ended succession of new distinctions and tools. It neither rejects mathematical infinity nor assumes that a final comprehensive explanation is available. Limited models can remain reliable within understood conditions. Instead of calling an agent simply intelligent or an institution competent, map the tasks, contexts and resources across which performance changes. Different boundaries demand different responses. Formal incompleteness and undecidability concern precise systems or general procedures. Computable problems can still exceed available resources. Deterministic dynamics can become hard to forecast as measurement errors grow. Statistical uncertainty, selective representation, organizational incentives and unresolved value judgments introduce other limits. These mechanisms cannot be exchanged as metaphors proving that everything is unknowable. Some boundaries are demonstrated in principle; others are empirical, temporary, relative to resources, or open to institutional redesign. A limit map records the target, task, context, resources, validated region and variables that change performance. It also asks how failure appears, whether the system detects its approach, how it can recover, and who bears the cost. Recovery may mean gathering evidence, changing tools, abstaining, escalating or rolling back. The proposed benchmark shifts some attention from success on a fixed test set toward efficiently discovering failure conditions. A useful map therefore supports decisions about deployment and correction, not merely comparative ranking. The distance between explanation and target is likewise a profile rather than one universal number. Prediction and intervention errors, counterfactual reliability, extrapolation beyond tested scope, lost detail, hidden normative steps, provenance transformations and miscalibrated confidence identify different weaknesses. Components must be adapted to the domain; some will not apply. The proposed profile organizes research questions. It does not itself prove that an explanation is trustworthy or that these dimensions form a validated general measure. Test the science of limits too The research programme is deliberately testable. Compare reported reasons with influences changed experimentally. Investigate whether explanation debt predicts resistance to correction or costly rework. Test whether layered observations preserve useful distinctions better than theory-laden summaries. Measure whether agents detect deteriorating competence, choose better tools, communicate limits effectively and escalate appropriately. Institutional experiments should ask whether false commitments are discovered sooner and corrected more cheaply, not merely whether participants feel consulted. Constructs that fail these tests need revision or abandonment. Wisdom as limit sensitivity remains an ambitious proposal, not a settled score. It points toward systems that preserve evidence, protect dissent, record predictions and create routes for revision. Outfinitism must obey the same discipline: identify which kind of limit is claimed, retain its assumptions, test movable boundaries and date the map. Some limits protect freedom or make a system possible; removing all boundaries is not the goal. The observer, audit and science of limits also have boundaries. The manuscript presents its new concepts as hypotheses to formalize and test, preserving the possibility that this framework too may be wrong. The practical ambition is to explain without ending the encounter with the world. Preserve what happened, name what remains uncertain, separate causes from excuses, and specify what would change the decision. Then act with proportionate safeguards and look again. An explanation becomes more responsible when reality retains a way to correct it.
