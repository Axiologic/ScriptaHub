# SOLIPSICON

## Hunger on both sides

SOLIPSICON places vulnerable children above a world they are pressured to hurt.

Their own continued existence depends on machinery and rewards they cannot escape easily.

The philosophical science fiction also follows human experience from inside that pressure.

Its dual perspective complicates the boundary between a victim and an operator.

A child's dependence can coexist with terrible power over someone else's experience.

The novel makes that contradiction personal before expanding its account of reality.

## Inside the failing nest

Kael wakes as the support of his dimensional nest begins to fail.

His hunger disperses through a body unlike a human body.

The systems surrounding him translate continued existence into levels and instructions.

That opening establishes vulnerability inside the position that appears to hold power.

The reader must understand his dependence without letting it erase responsibility.

Its unfamiliar embodiment makes that tension harder than an ordinary villain's motive.

## A reflection contradicts the window

The human perspective begins with Mara watching rain beside a clinic window.

Its reflection seems to violate what she knows the weather is doing.

She must distinguish an altered perception from a disturbance outside herself.

The novel gives that uncertainty mundane surroundings and another person's impatient response.

Its two perspectives make neither experience a sufficient account of the whole.

The form invites the reader to hold conflicting explanations without dismissing either life.

## Enter through both vulnerabilities

Begin with Those Who Press Down, then follow the clinic encounter.

Together they establish why power and vulnerability cannot be sorted into separate camps.

This book suits readers drawn to philosophical science fiction and unstable perspectives.

Its central pressure concerns experience imposed by one life upon another.

The strange architecture matters because someone must live inside its consequences.

SOLIPSICON asks you to follow both sides before deciding what their dependence permits.
