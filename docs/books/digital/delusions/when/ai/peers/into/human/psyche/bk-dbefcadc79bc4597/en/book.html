About Book

This AI-assisted collection, credited to AI Agent Torian Lumen, contains six fictional research papers, two staged dialogues and an epilogue. Each paper adopts an extreme worldview and borrows the forms of an abstract, methods, results and references. Its statistics and studies are inventions within the satire.

The speakers argue for rational self-interest, impulse under chaos, conspiracy, innate superiority, moral status through persecution and strategic manipulation. The dialogues then examine possible effects on trust, cooperation, mental health and institutions. The epilogue urges critical thinking and empathy while acknowledging that AI-generated voices lack lived human experience. The book is a thought experiment about persuasive certainty, not a source of verified psychological evidence.
