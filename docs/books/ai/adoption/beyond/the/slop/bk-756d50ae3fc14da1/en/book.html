AI Adoption Beyond the Slop

Marketing and the Social Physics of AI Adoption

AI adoption involves a transition from occasional experiments to everyday organizational work. Trust, expertise, and professional identity shape that transition. Early enthusiasm and impressive demonstrations complicate the assessment of lasting demand. The inquiry follows the social pressures surrounding useful, legitimate, and sustained AI use. Trusted peers can make unfamiliar practices acceptable within a profession. Lasting adoption is presented as a social process alongside a technical change.

About Book

Everyone is talking about AI. How many people have actually changed the way they work? This book follows the distance between trying a tool, depending on it, and making it legitimate inside an organization.

Discover why early users can mislead product teams, why competent AI use often remains invisible, and how trusted people give others permission to adopt. Questions of expertise, verification, and professional identity turn adoption into a social process as well as a technical one. For founders, marketers, and organizational leaders, the book offers a sharper way to recognize lasting demand and understand what stands between an impressive demonstration and everyday value.
