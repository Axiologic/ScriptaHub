About Book

A customer reports a broken laboratory instrument. Answering the email is easy; finding the account, checking telemetry, comparing firmware, and resolving the incident is the real work. This book starts with that difference between producing text and pursuing an outcome.

Follow AI agents through the engineering decisions that make useful action possible: tools, state, control flow, evaluation, and enterprise deployment. Concrete workflow situations reveal why autonomy changes both the value of a system and the ways it can fail. A guide for builders and technical teams who need to turn model capability into work that can be checked, maintained, and trusted.
