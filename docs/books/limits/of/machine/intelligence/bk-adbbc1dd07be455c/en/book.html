Limits of Machine Intelligence

An AI-assisted exploration of Outfinitism, moving frontiers, and the threshold of science

How should mathematical limits shape our judgment of actual AI systems? The inquiry connects impossibility theorems with finite tasks and limited resources. Outfinitism introduces questions about justification and moving boundaries of knowledge. Useful assessment remains in tension with the search for final certainty. The book distinguishes universal impossibility from justified judgments within concrete constraints. Its proposals remain provisional and open to criticism.

About Book

A theorem can rule out a perfect procedure for every possible case. What does that tell us about a particular machine, working on a finite task, with limited authority and a deadline? This book makes that distinction its starting point.

Through Outfinitism and the idea of moving frontiers, it investigates what bounded systems can justify without claiming final certainty. The AI-assisted inquiry keeps its own proposals provisional and asks readers to examine the limits of their warrant. For those interested in foundations, computation, and AI evaluation, it offers a more precise conversation about impossibility, useful knowledge, and improvement under real constraints.
