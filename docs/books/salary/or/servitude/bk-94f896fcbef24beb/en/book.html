About Book

Salary or Servitude? asks when a job that provides income begins to erode autonomy, health or purpose. Its opening satire exaggerates exhausting schedules and management control to expose assumptions hidden in ordinary workplace language. The book then traces changing relations between employers and workers, from dependent labor and factories to corporations, startups and gig platforms.

Later chapters examine burnout, limited agency and work measured by visible busyness rather than useful outcomes. They consider self-managed teams, flexible schedules, remote work and technology that can either support or surveil workers. The conclusion imagines organizations that reward learning, meaningful contribution and varied lives, while acknowledging that no single arrangement cures every problem.
