About Book

This book reads the relationship between supply and demand through Jean-Baptiste Say’s Traité d’économie politique. Its opening chapters place Say among classical economists and explain his emphasis on production and exchange. The entrepreneur then becomes central: someone who assembles resources, accepts uncertainty, recognizes unmet needs, and adjusts offerings to buyers.

A chapter on market dynamics examines price signals, changing preferences, subsidies, tariffs, technology, and economic cycles. Later discussion relates these ideas to global supply chains, digital commerce, the pandemic, and economic policy. The conclusion connects productive activity, purchasing power, entrepreneurial initiative, and social well-being. The bibliography preserves the works cited in the source.
