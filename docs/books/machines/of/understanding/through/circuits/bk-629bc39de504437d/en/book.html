Machines of Understanding Through Circuits

SOP Lang, Sufficient Representations, and Controlled Reasoning

This course develops a research programme for reasoning through circuits and sufficient representations. It introduces SOP Lang alongside mathematical tools for describing partial information. Chapters examine relations, information orders, abstract interpretation, and fixed points. Examples investigate correlations that individual circuit connections may fail to preserve. The discussion separates executing, verifying, and discovering a useful construction. Its central question asks what representations must retain so reasoning can continue.
