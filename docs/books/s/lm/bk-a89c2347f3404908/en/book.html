About Book

Why should one general model perform every part of an AI workflow? Interpreting a request, calculating a result, checking a permission, and remembering an outcome require different kinds of competence.

S*LM explores intelligence through division of labor among small, specialized, symbolic, and general components. The book follows language into action through routing, memory, validation, recovery, and escalation. It asks how much generality each role needs and where exact software should hold authority. For developers building agent systems, it offers an architectural alternative centered on allocating the right work to the right component and evaluating the whole process.
