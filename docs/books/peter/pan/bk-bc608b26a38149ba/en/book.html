Peter Pan

The Darling children, Neverland, and the choice to grow up

Peter visits the Darling nursery and invites Wendy, John, and Michael to fly away. In Neverland, the Lost Boys welcome Wendy into their underground home. Tinker Bell, Tiger Lily, mermaids, and pirates complicate the children’s adventures. Captain Hook threatens the children as Peter faces danger and responsibility. Wendy’s stories keep the memory of her family alive. Barrie closes with a return home and the passing of childhood between generations.

About Book

J. M. Barrie begins in the Darling nursery, where Peter’s lost shadow brings him into contact with Wendy, John, and Michael. Their flight takes them to Neverland, a place of Lost Boys, fairies, mermaids, pirates, and invented domestic life. Wendy tells stories and cares for the boys while Peter enjoys adventures without accepting the obligations she remembers from home.

The chapters move through danger at the lagoon, Captain Hook’s pursuit, the pirate ship, and the children’s choice to return. A final chapter follows Wendy into adulthood and watches another generation meet Peter. This edition also preserves Taler Vox’s source introduction as a distinct editorial perspective before Barrie’s seventeen chapters.
