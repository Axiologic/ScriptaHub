About Book

What would it mean for a machine to read a sentence without filling every gap with a plausible guess? Meaning as a Path starts with a fixed set of questions about a sentence: who acts, what happens, to whom, when, and under what condition. A missing answer remains missing. The result is a chart that can be inspected rather than an impression hidden in a fluent reply.

The book proposes a compact vector-symbolic reader that learns jumps between kinds of words and places found words into roles. It tests the idea in constructed worlds and question sets, then examines ambiguity, translation, robotics interlocks, regulated documents, and alignment with larger language models. The method is presented with its limits: it depends on the chosen questions, training examples, and domain boundaries.
