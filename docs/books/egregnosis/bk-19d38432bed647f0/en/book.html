About Book

A society can contain people who know something and still behave as though it cannot see it. How do private observations become shared reality, and why do some possibilities remain invisible until a sudden collective shift?

Egregnosis explores the distributed processes through which groups notice, interpret, and act. Attention, status, expectations, and channels of communication help form a collective picture that no individual fully controls. The brain metaphor opens questions about how this coordination works and where it breaks. For readers interested in social psychology, institutions, and cultural change, the book invites a closer look at the machinery of what everyone takes for granted.
