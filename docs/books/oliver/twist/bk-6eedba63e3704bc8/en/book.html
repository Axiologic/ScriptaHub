Oliver Twist

Themes of Oliver's childhood in the workhouse and his journey to London

Oliver grows up in a workhouse that treats poor children with cruelty. His escape to London leads him into Fagin's circle of thieves. Kindness appears alongside exploitation as adults compete to shape his future. Nancy's choices reveal the danger of trying to protect him. The mystery of Oliver's family unfolds amid pursuit and betrayal. Charles Dickens turns one child's story into a sharp portrait of poverty and social injustice.
