Is There Light at the End of the Tunnel?

The Antinomies of Power, the Limits of Freedom, and a Civilization Able to Correct Itself

This political essay asks why systems built for liberation can create new dependencies. It compares communist bureaucracy, capitalist privilege, democratic weakness, and concentrated information. The author separates empirical findings from hypotheses and institutional proposals. A constitutional cosmolocalism seeks distributed capacity without surrendering basic rights. Artificial intelligence and basic income are examined through ownership, control, and voice. The closing test asks whether institutions can resist capture and correct mistakes.
