About Book

An AI can name a theory, write equations, build software, and draft a skeptical review of its own work. The package may look like mature research before it has faced the independent checks that would earn that appearance.

This book investigates the Reality Gap between apparent progress and conclusions that survive contact with evidence. It explores executable inquiry and proposed guardians with limited authority to test knowledge claims and their purposes. For researchers and builders using generative AI, it offers a demanding question to carry into every polished result: what has actually been established, and what would show that it is wrong?
