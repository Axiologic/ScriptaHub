About Book

On Oriven, a damaged body can be repaired and a self can continue through living networks. Then an observatory records a predator killing prey on another planet. For the first time, a civilization built around repair witnesses a death it cannot reach.

The discovery unsettles everything Oriven believes about suffering, continuity, and care. As observation approaches intervention, questions of privacy and consent become entangled with first contact and scarcity at home. This speculative universe invites readers into minds and bodies unlike our own, while asking a recognizable question: when you gain the power to see another world's pain, what are you entitled to do about it?
