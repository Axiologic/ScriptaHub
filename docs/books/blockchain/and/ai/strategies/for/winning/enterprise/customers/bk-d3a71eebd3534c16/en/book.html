About Book

This practical report asks how a startup working with blockchain and AI might win large enterprise customers. It begins with direct relationships, identifying problems before building a complete product, and proposes customer-funded pilots and minimum viable products. Open-source tools, demonstrations, and a skilled team help make early proposals concrete. The discussion draws on lean startup, customer development, Jobs to Be Done, and other cited entrepreneurship literature.

A second proposal is a nonprofit professional community that shares knowledge, offers training, and collects membership fees while commercial offerings mature. The report considers prominent initial members, especially in pharmaceuticals, then turns to accelerators, online communities, and European research consortia. Its final chapter gathers practices for market feedback, partnerships, security and compliance, data analysis, innovation, recurring revenue, and networking. Funding percentages and membership figures are illustrative proposals, not established outcomes.
