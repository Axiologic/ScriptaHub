Cones of Meaning

Meta-Rationality, Outfinitism, and Alignment in the Age of Generative Intelligence

Abundant generated explanations raise questions about belief, purpose, and judgment. Cones of meaning describe the frames that make some questions feel natural. Meta-rationality, Outfinitism, and AI alignment meet around evidence and formal limits. The inquiry focuses on what shapes a question before an answer is produced. Frames determine what a method includes and what it leaves outside. Responsible judgment therefore involves examining the choices that make an answer possible.

About Book

When convincing explanations become cheap, deciding what deserves belief becomes harder. This book asks how people and institutions can keep their bearings in a world overflowing with generated arguments, plans, and interpretations.

Its image of cones of meaning explores how frames make certain questions and answers feel natural while pushing others out of view. Meta-rationality, Outfinitism, and AI alignment meet in a discussion of evidence, purpose, and the limits of formal methods. For readers drawn to philosophy and generative intelligence, the book offers tools for examining the choices that come before an answer: what matters, who decides, and what the method leaves outside.
