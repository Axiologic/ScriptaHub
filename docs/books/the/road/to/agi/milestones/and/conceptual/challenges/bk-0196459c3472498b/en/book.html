The Road to AGI: Milestones and Conceptual Challenges

Thirteen proposed capabilities on the path to general intelligence

Can language models reason beyond familiar patterns? This book begins with neurosymbolic logic and a proposed sleep-like phase for consolidating experience. It tests prediction against chaos, complexity, and limited pockets of computability. Specialized digital sciences lead toward theory-making, interdisciplinary transfer, and autonomous experiments. Later chapters examine changing goals, emotional understanding, self-improvement, situational ethics, and self-awareness. A final table pairs twelve phases with possible measurements and speculative post-2024 time ranges.

About Book

The author treats AGI as a sequence of capabilities that would need evidence, not as a single inevitable breakthrough. The book starts with limits of statistical language models, proposes neurosymbolic reasoning and offline consolidation, then asks how an AI should act where complexity defeats precise prediction.

The middle chapters move from digital scientific disciplines to competing theories, transfer between fields, experiments, and changing goals. The final chapters examine emotional behavior, recursive improvement, context-sensitive ethics, and self-awareness. The closing twelve-row table gives proposed tests and explicitly speculative ranges measured from 2024.
