The Network of Intent

How Personal AI Agents Could Rebuild Discovery, Trust, and Work

This investment thesis explores a network of agents representing personal and organizational intentions. It examines discovery problems in hiring, investment, research collaboration, and local services. Chapters investigate reciprocal qualification, evidence exchange, negotiation, and contextual reputation. The proposed infrastructure raises questions about privacy, fraud, decentralization, and human approval. Product and market discussions consider early adoption, operational demands, and network economics. Its central tension concerns effective representation without surrendering judgment or control.
