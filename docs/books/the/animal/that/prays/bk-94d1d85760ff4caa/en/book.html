About Book

What might religion look like to an observer who does not kneel? Through the imagined perspective of an alien AI, this book approaches prayer, ritual, belief, and belonging as parts of a complex human architecture.

Bodies synchronize, attention changes, fear finds a language, and communities bind themselves to stories larger than one life. The inquiry connects biology, power, moral order, and the possibility of transcendence while asking what an outside analysis can and cannot decide. For readers curious about religion and the human condition, it offers an unfamiliar vantage point from which to reconsider some of humanity's oldest and most persistent practices.
