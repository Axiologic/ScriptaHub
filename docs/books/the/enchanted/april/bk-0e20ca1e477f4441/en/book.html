The Enchanted April

Four women, an Italian garden, and a month away from ordinary life

Two London women answer an advertisement for an Italian castle. They invite two strangers to share its April rent. The four guests bring different hopes and private burdens. Sunlit gardens unsettle their habits and expectations. Visitors from home complicate their retreat. Von Arnim explores friendship, marriage, solitude, and the possibility of change.

About Book

In Elizabeth von Arnim’s novel, Lotty Wilkins finds an advertisement for a castle on the Italian coast and persuades Rose Arbuthnot to share the cost of a month away. They recruit Mrs. Fisher and Lady Caroline Dester, whose reasons for leaving England differ sharply from their own.

At San Salvatore, beauty and quiet offer room to reconsider marriage, public attention, age, solitude, and companionship. The four women do not react alike, and visits from home test what their retreat has changed. Von Arnim makes the garden more than scenery: it becomes a setting for quiet observation, comic misunderstanding, and new conversations.
