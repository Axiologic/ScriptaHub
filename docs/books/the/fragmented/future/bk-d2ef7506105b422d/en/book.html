The Fragmented Future

AI, City-Regions, and New Forms of Human Civilization

The Fragmented Future explores political organization under expanding AI capabilities. Smaller cities and regions might gain powers once reserved for large bureaucracies. States and platforms could also acquire stronger instruments of control. Scenarios of city-regions and federations connect local autonomy with shared obligations. Rights, defense and infrastructure complicate any simple celebration of fragmentation. The central message treats the distribution of power as a constitutional choice, not a technological destiny.

About Book

AI could help smaller cities and regions manage complexity once reserved for large bureaucracies. It could also give states and platforms unprecedented powers of coordination and control. Which political future follows is still a choice.

This book explores city-regions, federations, changing scarcity, and new forms of authority through scenarios rather than a single forecast. Local autonomy is examined alongside common rights, defense, infrastructure, and responsibility for wider consequences. For readers interested in governance after technological change, it offers a constitutional question: how should powers be distributed so that smaller communities gain room to act without losing the protections of a shared world?
