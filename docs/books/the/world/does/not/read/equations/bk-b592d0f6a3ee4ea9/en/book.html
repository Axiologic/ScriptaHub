The World Does Not Read Equations

Natural Laws, Emergence, and the Limits of Understanding

This essay asks what natural laws are and how explanations acquire their authority. It connects scientific models with philosophical questions about order and reality. Chapters explore emergence, the origin of regularities, and the limits of understanding. The possibility of a simulated world becomes a test of competing descriptions. Thought experiments distinguish observed patterns from claims about underlying mechanisms. Its guiding question concerns the relationship between the world and the equations describing it.
