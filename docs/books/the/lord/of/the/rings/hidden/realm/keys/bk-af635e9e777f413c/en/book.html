About Book

This original Middle-earth story begins when Bilbo Baggins finds an old map among forgotten possessions at Bag End. Gandalf connects the map to a growing threat, and Bilbo looks for companions rather than setting out alone. Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, Frodo, and Sam join a fellowship whose different strengths become important on the road.

Rough terrain, storms, and hostile forces test their resolve before they reach a concealed realm and an ancient pedestal. The fourth chapter brings the group into conflict with servants of Sauron and presents light as a force strengthened by trust and unity. The conclusion interprets the “keys” as courage, friendship, sacrifice, and hope. The bibliography preserves the source’s references to Tolkien’s books and film adaptations.
