About Book

This guide examines Richard K. Morgan’s Altered Carbon through the technologies and social conflicts of its fictional universe. It begins with cortical stacks, resleeving and the Rawling virus, then asks whether a stored or copied mind remains the same person.

The chapters connect changing bodies with memory, gender, status and prejudice. They follow the unequal access to replacement bodies and the extraordinary longevity of the wealthy Meths. The final chapters consider consent, punishment, artificial intelligence and Takeshi Kovacs’s investigation of Laurens Bancroft’s death.
