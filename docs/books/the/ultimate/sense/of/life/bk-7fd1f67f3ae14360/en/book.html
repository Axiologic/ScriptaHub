About Book

When we ask for the meaning of life, are we asking why organisms exist, what deserves our effort, how to endure suffering, or whether the universe has a purpose? One answer can quietly pretend to settle several different questions.

This serious comedy moves among gods, ants, consciousness, saints, and ordinary lives to explore what meaning could require. Its philosophical wager imagines life as a process through which existence can experience and care about itself. For readers who enjoy large questions with room for doubt and humor, the book offers an invitation to think about significance without waiting for the universe to provide a final explanation.
