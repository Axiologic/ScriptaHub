The Call of the Wild

Buck’s journey from a California estate to the Yukon wilderness

Buck begins as a cherished dog on Judge Miller’s California estate. A betrayal sends him into the Klondike gold rush and the world of sled teams. He learns new rules from handlers, rivals, hunger, and northern weather. John Thornton offers a bond different from the force Buck has known. The edition pairs London’s complete seven-chapter novel with historical context and character analysis. Its questions concern survival, loyalty, adaptation, and the pull between civilization and wilderness.

About Book

Jack London’s 1903 novel follows Buck from a protected California life into the demands of the northern trail. The delivered edition retains the complete seven-chapter narrative and a substantive introductory perspective on London, the gold rush, historical context, characters, and narrative threads.

Buck encounters capable and incompetent owners, learns the law of club and fang, struggles for leadership, and discovers a deep attachment to John Thornton. The story tests how instinct, labor, violence, and affection reshape identity under changing conditions.
