About Book

A patient loses treatment, a worker loses a job, a family loses assistance. The harm is clear, but each participant can truthfully point to a limited role in the process. Who is responsible when no single intention contains the injury?

This book follows the maze of institutions, incentives, contractors, and automated decisions that can produce victims without a visible persecutor. It asks who knew, who could intervene, who benefited, and who can repair. For readers concerned with complex organizations and the future of AI, it explores how responsibility might be rebuilt around distributed power without inventing a single villain or letting everyone disappear.
