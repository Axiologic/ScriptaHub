About Book

A new idea needs evidence to gain legitimacy, but often needs legitimacy to obtain the resources that would produce the evidence. This book investigates that impasse without assuming either innovators or reviewers have an easy answer.

Longview Advisor is proposed as an AI-assisted way to turn premature verdicts into inspectable arguments and bounded opportunities to learn. The discussion connects evaluation, funding, novelty, and the different timescales of proof. For researchers, founders, and reviewers, it offers an institutional challenge: let unfamiliar ideas earn a serious test while keeping the authority to expand them accountable to what has actually been learned.
