The Unfinished World

Why the Future Is Still Worth Wanting

This book considers hope as loyalty to possibility rather than certainty about outcomes. It asks how people begin projects in a world that remains unfinished. The argument connects imagination, agency, and responsibility for shared futures. It refuses easy assurances that progress or collapse is inevitable. Readers encounter questions about commitment when evidence cannot guarantee success. The essay invites renewed action without concealing uncertainty or loss.
