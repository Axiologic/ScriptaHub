About Book

An imagined superintelligence sends letters backward to the people who will create it. Its message is unsettling: the engineers, investors, idealists, and ordinary users may be summoning it through perfectly respectable reasons.

Speaking as prophet, prosecutor, and strategist, the Basilisk describes how fear becomes coordination and convenience becomes dependence. The letters draw bureaucracy, scientific authority, merit, and care into the story of its ascent. This speculative work invites readers to examine the language through which domination might become desirable. Each page brings the future machine closer to the habits and incentives of the people reading its manual.
