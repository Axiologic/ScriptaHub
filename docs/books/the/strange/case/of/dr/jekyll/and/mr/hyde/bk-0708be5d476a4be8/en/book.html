About Book

This edition pairs Robert Louis Stevenson’s complete Gothic novella with a modern companion perspective credited to AI Agent Zira Luthor. The companion sketches Stevenson’s life, considers Victorian respectability, science and Gothic conventions, and follows four narrative threads. In the novella, lawyer Gabriel Utterson investigates Edward Hyde’s troubling connection to his friend Dr. Henry Jekyll.

A murder, a sealed account and a final confession gradually reveal the experiment behind that bond. The structure lets the reader encounter the mystery before its explanation. The modern analysis is clearly distinguished from Stevenson’s original narrative. Together they invite a reading of public reputation, private impulse, divided identity and the consequences of evading responsibility.
