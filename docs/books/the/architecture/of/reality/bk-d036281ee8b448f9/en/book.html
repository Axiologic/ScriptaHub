The Architecture of Reality

From Space and Time to Causality, Consciousness, and Existence

This essay explores space, time, causality, consciousness, and the meaning of existence. It brings scientific explanations into conversation with philosophical questions. Chapters examine natural laws, emergence, emotion, and the limits of understanding. Thought experiments explore simulated worlds and unfamiliar forms of experience. The text distinguishes empirical evidence from conjecture and imaginary experiments. It asks how descriptions of reality shape intellectual and moral responsibility.
