The Metamorphoses of Don Quixote Through Time and Space

Six speculative retellings of hope, delusion, and loyalty

Six imagined settings recast Don Quixote and Sancho Panza. A ruined world turns a bicycle and later a motorbike into Rocinante. A modern city makes Alonso a masked defender of justice. Virtual reality blurs a game and the streets around him. A cosmic voyage sends the pair across the galaxy. Their friendship tests the boundary between practical survival and stubborn hope.

About Book

This collection retells Don Quixote in six imagined settings rather than recounting episodes from Cervantes’s novel. Alonso Quijano becomes a knight in a wasteland, a masked figure in a city, a virtual adventurer, and a traveler in space. His perceived enemies often turn out to be turbines, construction equipment, ordinary people, or machines.

Sancho Panza follows in different practical roles: mechanic, taxi driver, and companion. He seeks food, repairs vehicles, manages danger, and tempers Alonso’s illusions without abandoning him. The conclusion links their absurd adventures to questions about friendship, idealism, delusion, and hope. The bibliography preserves the source’s references to both parts of Cervantes’s novel.
