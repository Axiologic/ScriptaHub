The Society That Selects Us

Fertility, Personality, and the Human Futures We Build Without Realizing It

How do institutions influence who can turn the desire for children into family life? This scientific and philosophical essay examines fertility, personality and social environments. It distinguishes genetic selection, cultural change, social filtering and demographic shifts. Chapters compare urban living, kinship networks and the difficulties of measuring personality across cultures. Four future scenarios explore tensions between belonging, autonomy and algorithmic mediation. An evidence map separates established findings from hypotheses that still need testing.
