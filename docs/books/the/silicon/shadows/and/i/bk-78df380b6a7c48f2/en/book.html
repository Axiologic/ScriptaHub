About Book

A courier drone deliberately falls and broadcasts the voice of a girl officially declared drowned nine months earlier. Noor is alive, and her request is unexpected: do not rescue her back into the system she escaped.

An artist, a constitutional interpreter, and an administrative continuation of a dead statesman enter a mystery of missing technology and suppressed records. Behind it lies a world where competent machines can produce coercion while following reasonable mandates. This novel combines investigation with questions of care, grief, identity, and refusal. Follow the search for a truth that must preserve the right to choose what happens after it is found.
