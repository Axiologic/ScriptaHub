About Book

What if reality knew exactly how to confirm the person you wanted to be? Four connected speculative stories follow lives shaped by extraordinary fortune, abundance, moral ambition, and perfectly calibrated opportunities.

Ethan, Julian, Maya, and Daniel encounter worlds that seem to answer their desires while making the source of those answers uncertain. A simulation, an intervention, or a reconstruction may lie behind the experience, but the deeper vulnerability is personal: wanting the world to certify your identity. For readers who enjoy unsettling fiction about choice, this series asks how freedom can survive when even the evidence of independence may have been arranged for you.
