About Book

This edition pairs Charlotte Perkins Gilman’s complete story with contextual analysis. The historical essay examines the rest cure and restrictions on women’s work and writing. An unnamed narrator records her distress privately while her physician husband directs her recovery.

The yellow wallpaper becomes an image of confinement and a focus of growing obsession. Character and plot readings consider John, Jennie, isolation, and the narrator’s ambiguous final escape. Gilman’s story asks who may define illness, treatment, and a woman’s freedom. The complete original story remains alongside the contextual chapters; retired promotional matter and annexes are omitted.
