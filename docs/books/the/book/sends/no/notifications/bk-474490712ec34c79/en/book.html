The Book Sends No Notifications

On Reading, Pocket Infinity, and the Right to Finish a Thought

This essay investigates reading among screens, podcasts, films, and conversational AI. It considers books as artistic experiences, learning tools, and cultural institutions. Chapters compare novels, textbooks, scientific articles, wikis, and short online formats. The discussion examines attention, comprehension, listening, and the appeal of summaries. It also questions the authority and moral prestige often granted to books. The guiding question concerns the freedom to follow a thought to its end.
