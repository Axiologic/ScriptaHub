The Right to Copy

Copyright, Trademarks, and Creative Work in the Age of AI

This course explores copyright, trademarks, and creative work in the age of AI. It compares international foundations with legal differences across several jurisdictions. Book rewriting and software reimplementation provide recurring practical cases. Chapters distinguish expression, function, reputation, licensing, and the public domain. The discussion also examines provenance, enforcement, and the limits of technical protection. Its central question asks what creators can protect when equivalent results can be produced differently.
