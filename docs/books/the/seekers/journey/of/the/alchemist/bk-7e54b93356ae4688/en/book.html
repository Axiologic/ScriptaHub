The Seeker’s Journey of the Alchemist

Santiago’s dream, desert lessons, and inner treasure

A merchant’s unfulfilled pilgrimage exposes fear of both failure and fulfillment. The desert, an Englishman, Fatima, and an alchemist shape his understanding of belief. At the pyramids, bandits reveal where the material treasure actually lies. The four chapters distinguish buried gold from the wisdom gained along the journey.
