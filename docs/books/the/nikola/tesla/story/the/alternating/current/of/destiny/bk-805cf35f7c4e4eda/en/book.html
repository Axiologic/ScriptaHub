The Nikola Tesla Story: The Alternating Current of Destiny

Alternating current, invention and the life behind the electrical age

Nikola Tesla’s polyphase system helped make long-distance electrical distribution practical. His induction motors and Niagara Falls installation connect laboratory ideas to public infrastructure. Experiments with high frequencies, remote control and wireless transmission widened his ambitions. The conflict with Edison joined engineering, business interests and public persuasion. Wardenclyffe, patent disputes and financial struggles reveal the limits of visionary invention. The book follows Tesla’s philosophy, family influences and solitude alongside his achievements.
