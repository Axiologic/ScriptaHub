Art, observation, invention, and the myths surrounding Leonardo

About Book

This portrait follows Leonardo da Vinci as a painter, inventor, scientist, and thinker. It opens with his mirror writing and asks whether that habit really signalled secrecy. The central chapters examine paintings, anatomical drawings, mechanical designs, and studies of plants, rocks, light, and mathematics.

They present the notebooks as a place where observation and imagination meet. Another chapter considers the mythology surrounding hidden messages, secret societies, and the Mona Lisa while noting the lack of reliable support for many claims. The closing discussion connects Leonardo’s methods to current work in medicine, engineering, art conservation, and design. An unrelated Edison passage in the source has been excluded from this edition.
