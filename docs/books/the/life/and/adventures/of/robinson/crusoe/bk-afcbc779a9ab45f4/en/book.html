The Life and Adventures of Robinson Crusoe

Themes of Crusoe's voyage and life on the island

Robinson Crusoe ignores his father's advice and follows the sea. A shipwreck leaves him alone on an island for many years. He builds shelter, grows food and keeps a record of survival. Signs of other people change his sense of safety and solitude. His meeting with Friday brings companionship and difficult questions about power. Daniel Defoe's adventure joins practical ingenuity to fear, faith and colonial assumptions.
