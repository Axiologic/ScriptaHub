About Book

The biography follows Elon Musk from his first internet company through PayPal, SpaceX, Tesla, and later ventures. It describes the sale of Zip2, the rise of online payments, and the capital those successes supplied for new risks. SpaceX’s failed early launches and later orbital milestones form one major thread; Tesla’s vehicles, battery production, and financial pressures form another.

The final chapters introduce The Boring Company, Neuralink, OpenAI, and Starlink. Alongside commercial and technical achievements, the book discusses leadership disputes, public controversy, and the difference between demonstrated results and future ambitions. Its claims reflect the original 2024 manuscript.
