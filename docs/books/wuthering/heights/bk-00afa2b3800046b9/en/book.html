Wuthering Heights

Catherine, Heathcliff, and the legacy of two houses

Lockwood’s visit to Wuthering Heights opens a layered account of Catherine Earnshaw and Heathcliff. Nelly Dean traces their childhood bond, Catherine’s marriage to Edgar Linton, and Heathcliff’s return. His revenge moves through property, marriage, and the lives of the next generation. The Yorkshire moors frame conflicting desires, social rank, grief, and confinement. Catherine Linton and Hareton Earnshaw find a different way to relate after inherited harm. Brontë’s shifting narrators leave passion and cruelty open to the reader’s judgment.
