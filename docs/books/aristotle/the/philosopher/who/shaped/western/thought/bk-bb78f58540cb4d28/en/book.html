Aristotle: The Philosopher Who Shaped Western Thought

Observation, virtue, causation and logic in Aristotle's thought

Aristotle studies animals through observation, dissection and classification. His natural philosophy explains motion, elements and change through four causes. Virtue ethics connects cultivated character, practical wisdom and human flourishing. His political analysis compares constitutions and asks how citizens pursue the common good. Metaphysics examines substance, potentiality, actuality and the Unmoved Mover. Categorical propositions and syllogisms show how his logic structures deduction.
