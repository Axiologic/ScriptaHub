Marx’s Lessons on Capitalism

Commodities, surplus value and class relations in modern economies

The book explains Marx’s distinction between use-value and exchange-value through everyday commodities. It follows money into capital and asks how ownership shapes work and power. Surplus value links wages, profit and the distribution of rewards from production. Gig work and digital markets provide contemporary examples of precarious labor. Crises, globalization and environmental harm reveal tensions within capitalist growth. The conclusion examines worker organization, cooperatives and the search for a fairer economy.
