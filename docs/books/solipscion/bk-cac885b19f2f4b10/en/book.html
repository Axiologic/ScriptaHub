About Book

The adults have vanished. Six children survive in fragile chambers connected by a damaged structure, while a Tutor orders them into smaller worlds to serve desire and learn consequence. Refusal costs food and breathable space.

KERA and the others discover that the longing of lives below sustains their own. Every intervention is already compromised: help can feed them, but so can an unfulfilled wish. This science-fiction novel turns imagined worlds into a stark education in responsibility. Follow children trying to grow up inside a system that makes other people's desires necessary for survival, before they even understand who built it.
