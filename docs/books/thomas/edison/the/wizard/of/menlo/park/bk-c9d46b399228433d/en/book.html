Thomas Edison: The Wizard of Menlo Park

Laboratory teamwork, invention and the contested legacy of Edison

Edison's Menlo Park laboratory made invention a coordinated process of testing and refinement. His telegraph work helped form habits of experiment and practical problem-solving. Electric lighting required a power system as well as a workable lamp. The phonograph and moving-picture devices reshaped recorded sound and film. Patents, markets and collaborators complicate the image of a solitary genius. The book weighs technical achievements against disputes over competition and credit.
