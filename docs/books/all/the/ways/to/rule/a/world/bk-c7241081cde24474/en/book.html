About Book

Democracy, monarchy, technocracy: familiar labels can hide more than they reveal. What changes when political systems are examined through the mechanisms that authorize power, represent people, administer decisions, and correct mistakes?

This book connects the history of sovereignty with an exploration of political futures. It treats institutions as arrangements that solve problems of coordination while creating new opportunities for domination. Readers interested in history, governance, and institutional design will find a vocabulary for comparing systems beneath their slogans. Follow the argument from the origins of organized rule to the question every future society must answer: how can power remain open to correction?
