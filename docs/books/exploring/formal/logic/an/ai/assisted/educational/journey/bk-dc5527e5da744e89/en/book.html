Exploring Formal Logic: An AI Assisted Educational Journey

From truth tables to modal logic and AI

Nine chapters trace formal logic from ancient argument patterns to contemporary computing. Truth tables test validity, tautologies, and the structure of propositional arguments. Predicates and quantifiers express properties, relationships, existence, and generality. Modal and nonclassical systems examine necessity, time, uncertainty, and inconsistent information. Later chapters connect logical methods with mathematics, programming, AI, and ethics. The AI-assisted text invites critical scrutiny of its examples and formal claims.

About Book

This educational survey moves from propositions and proof rules to predicates, possible worlds, and alternative logics. Worked examples show where formalization clarifies an argument and where a system reaches its limits.

The later chapters examine mathematical foundations, automated reasoning, software verification, and philosophical questions. The reader can compare logical tools across disciplines while checking the assumptions behind each formal model.
