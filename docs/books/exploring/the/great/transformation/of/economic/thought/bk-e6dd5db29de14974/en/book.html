About Book

This book uses Karl Polanyi’s The Great Transformation to explore how markets and society shape one another. Its first chapter moves from cooperative, pre-market communities to the idea of a self-regulating market. The second considers the social costs of treating labor, land, and money as commodities, including alienation and vulnerability during crises.

A third chapter follows responses such as unions, social insurance, public health programs, and competition rules. The final chapter asks how economic freedom might coexist with worker protection, environmental standards, social enterprises, and civic participation. The conclusion returns to the claim that economic systems are human choices grounded in social values. A bibliography lists works by Smith, Hayek, Mises, Friedman, and Keynes.
