About Book

This guide begins with the workings and limits of large language models. It explains perplexity, sentence variation, fluency and specificity as different ways to examine generated text. Chapters on prompt design, tone and fine-tuning show how the same information can be shaped for customers, students or professional readers.

Later chapters imagine an AI-assisted science-fiction tool, adapt prose into screenplay dialogue, experiment with fragmented narration and follow reader choices through branching stories. The final chapter discusses possible future systems and questions of credit and ownership. These proposals reflect a 2024 perspective; the repeated practical requirement is human checking of facts, context and ethical consequences.
