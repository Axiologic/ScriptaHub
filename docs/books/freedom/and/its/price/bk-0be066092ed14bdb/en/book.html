Freedom and Its Price

Choice, Responsibility, and the Costs Borne by Others

This essay investigates freedom through choice, responsibility, and unequal power. It asks who can act and who bears the consequences of other people’s decisions. Philosophical arguments meet scientific questions and ordinary social situations. The discussion considers the relationship between personal autonomy and collective obligations. Institutions and unequal opportunities complicate apparently simple accounts of individual choice. The central tension concerns the conditions and costs of living freely together.
