About Book

We care for the tree we can touch. What happens to the world beyond its shade? This book uses the garden and the jungle to examine how local loyalty, cooperation, and moral certainty can leave distant costs unseen.

Its deliberately uncomfortable argument moves through power, memory, empathy, institutions, and artificial intelligence. Can cooperation create a larger predator? Can the language of goodness protect decisions from scrutiny? The investigation tests these darker possibilities before turning its criticism on its own assumptions. For readers willing to examine the limits of moral stories, it offers a demanding exploration of selective care and responsibility for the future.
