About Book

If we can imagine robots building shelters on Mars, why is secure housing on Earth still beyond so many people's reach? This book turns the ambition of space settlement into a question about priorities here at home.

Beginning with humanity's images of paradise, it connects water, safety, work, and shelter with the technologies and institutions that could make life more hospitable. Gardens offer protection, but their gates also raise questions about belonging and control. For readers drawn to ecological futures and social imagination, the book explores what it would take to build abundance that strengthens people's freedom as well as their security.
