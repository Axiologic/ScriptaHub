About Book

On Arua, a world divided between permanent daylight and darkness, civilisation survives along a narrow twilight belt. The Nivari have built an enduring peace around carefully managed resources, births and fear. Beneath the calm of their cities, however, the institutions that promise protection are also deciding who may belong and what truths can be spoken.

Follow demographer Sava Ilen into a society where population records, family loyalties and political power cannot be separated. This science-fiction novel asks what happens when creatures shaped by flight begin to question the rules of safety. Its alien world turns familiar tensions between security, freedom and responsibility into a story of choices with intimate consequences.

As Taren learns to stand his ground and Nemi learns to preserve what others would forget, their family becomes entangled in the making of a new order. Ecology, inherited fear and the allure of heroism shape a story spanning generations. For readers drawn to political science fiction, this is an invitation to ask who pays for peace—and what a society teaches its children to become.
