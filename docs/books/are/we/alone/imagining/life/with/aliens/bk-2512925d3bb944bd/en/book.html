Are We Alone? Imagining Life with Aliens

Searching for life beyond Earth and imagining contact

Exoplanets and habitable zones give the search for alien life possible destinations. TRAPPIST-1 illustrates both promising planetary systems and the limits of distant observations. Earth's extremophiles widen the range of environments where organisms might survive. Spectroscopy, space telescopes and rovers seek clues without turning biosignatures into proof. The book considers how discovery could change culture, politics and human identity. It asks readers to approach possible contact with curiosity, humility and care.
