About Book

Giving a model a handful of relevant passages is only one part of building an answer grounded in evidence. Sources must be chosen, parsed, retrieved, compared, and represented before generation even begins.

This book follows retrieval-augmented generation as a complete flow of information, showing how each stage shapes quality, security, and reproducibility. It then explores Evidence Portfolio Retrieval as a proposed approach to selecting collections of evidence for research. For developers and researchers building knowledge systems, it offers a vocabulary for locating failures and a testable direction for moving beyond document similarity toward the evidence a question actually requires.
