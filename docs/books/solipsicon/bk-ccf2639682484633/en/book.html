About Book

In fragile nests above the human world, children survive by entering minds and producing consequential events. The Loom rewards resonance, without explaining whether justice has anything to do with the score.

Kael-4 learns to optimize because results keep him alive. Vanya-Null finds it harder to treat human suffering as a neutral resource. Below them, people begin to sense that their intentions may not be entirely their own. This philosophical science-fiction novel follows both sides of an unequal encounter, asking what responsibility, resistance, and cognitive sovereignty can mean when exploitation has become the infrastructure of survival.
