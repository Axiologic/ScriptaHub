Moby Dick Or The Whale

Themes of Ishmael's voyage aboard the Pequod and Captain Ahab's pursuit

Ishmael boards the Pequod seeking work and a view of the whaling world. Captain Ahab makes the hunt for a white whale the ship's true purpose. The crew follows him across oceans while warning signs accumulate. Descriptions of whales and shipboard labor interrupt and deepen the voyage. Ahab's pursuit tests loyalty, judgment and the limits of human control. Herman Melville blends adventure, inquiry and tragedy in an unusually expansive sea novel.
