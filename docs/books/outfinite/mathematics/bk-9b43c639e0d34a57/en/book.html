Outfinite Mathematics

A Research Programme for Executable Science, Resource-Aware Mathematics, and Meta-Rational Foundations

Outfinite Mathematics explores mathematical foundations through finite resources and open boundaries. It asks how rules relate to their possible realizations. The programme makes operational limits part of mathematical inquiry itself. Its ambition is rigorous reasoning with explicit scope and room for further formalization.

About Book

Mathematics gains power by working beyond the limits of any particular machine. What changes when resource limits, kinds of existence, and the boundary between a rule and its realizations become part of the formal inquiry?

Outfinite Mathematics presents a research programme connecting foundations, computation, and executable science. It explores finite justification, open frontiers, and plural mathematical tools while identifying proposals that still require formalization and independent testing. For readers interested in philosophy of mathematics and machine reasoning, the book opens a set of questions about how rigorous methods can retain their strength while making their scope and operational limits explicit.
