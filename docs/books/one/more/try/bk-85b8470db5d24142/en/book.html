One More Try

Chance, Control, and the Illusion of Progress in the Age of AI

This essay follows repeated attempts from games of chance to conversations with AI. It examines uncertainty, perceived control, near misses, and the promise of another try. Examples connect poker and strategy games with discovery and programming agents. The discussion distinguishes encouragement, persuasive explanations, and independently verifiable progress. Chapters also consider emotional involvement, confirmation, and the limits of clinical evidence. Its recurring question concerns what remains after enthusiasm and repeated attempts.
