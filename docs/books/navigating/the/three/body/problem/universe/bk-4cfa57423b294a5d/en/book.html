About Book

The book follows the scientific, political and philosophical questions raised by Liu Cixin’s Three-Body Problem universe. It begins with sophons and their power to obstruct research, then examines how ideology influences scientific choices and responses to an alien threat. Human divisions, the Wallfacer Project and the Trisolaran civilization become tests of survival and moral judgment.

Later chapters compare historical cycles with adaptability and examine the assumptions behind the Dark Forest theory. Selected events from the trilogy give these ideas narrative force. A final chapter considers the work’s influence on science fiction and its continuing questions about knowledge, fear and humanity’s place in the cosmos.
