Top 10 Ways to Improve Emotional Intelligence (EQ)

Ten habits for awareness, empathy and thoughtful decisions

The book treats emotional intelligence as a set of habits that can improve through practice. Self-awareness begins with naming feelings, noticing triggers and reflecting on recurring patterns. Empathy grows through active listening and attention to perspectives beyond one’s own. Pauses, breathing and reframing help turn emotional triggers into deliberate responses. Adaptability, motivation and optimism support relationships and resilience when circumstances change. Precise emotional language, ethical decisions and feedback sustain continued EQ development.
