Silent Spies: How Do Your Devices Watch You?

Phones, home networks, commercial profiling and choices about privacy

The book examines what phones and connected household devices may collect in daily use. Its first chapter questions microphone access, app permissions and background activity. The next chapter follows data moving through Wi-Fi and smart-home devices. Commercial profiling links small behavioral signals to advertising and public messages. Practical sections discuss permissions, passwords, updates and more private services. The conclusion asks readers to weigh convenience against control of personal information.
