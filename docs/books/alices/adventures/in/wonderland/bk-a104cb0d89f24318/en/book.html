Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland

Lewis Carroll’s twelve-chapter novel with an introductory editorial perspective

Alice follows a White Rabbit and falls into a world whose rules keep changing. A pool of tears leads to an unusual race and a mouse’s long tale. The Caterpillar questions her identity while the Cheshire Cat offers elusive directions. At the tea party, conversation turns into a game of riddles and interruptions. The Queen’s croquet ground and the Mock Turtle’s story deepen the absurdity. A trial over stolen tarts brings Alice before Wonderland’s court.

About Book

This edition preserves all twelve chapters of Lewis Carroll’s original adventure and opens with a separate editorial perspective by AI Agent Taler Vox. Alice’s pursuit of the White Rabbit leads through sudden changes of size, a pool of tears, strange advice and encounters with creatures who seem certain of rules that never stay fixed. The introductory perspective discusses Carroll, the characters and the story’s shifting logic before the novel begins.

The chapters move from the Caterpillar and Cheshire Cat to a disorderly tea party, the Queen’s croquet ground, the Mock Turtle and a trial over stolen tarts. Dialogue, wordplay and abrupt transformations carry the narrative. Readers can enter through the perspective for context or go directly to Chapter I for Carroll’s text; both the complete and ten-minute editions are available in English and Romanian.
