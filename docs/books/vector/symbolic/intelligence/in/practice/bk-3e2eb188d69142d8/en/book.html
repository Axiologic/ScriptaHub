About Book

Can vector-symbolic representations support useful memory, reasoning and language capabilities? This experimental research book follows fifteen human-directed, AI-assisted repositories through implementations, reruns and diagnostic probes. It examines how binding, bundling and similarity behave in working systems, including the gaps between an elegant mathematical interpretation and what an actual decoder preserves.

Explore interference-aware memory, structured representations, abductive discovery, executable transformations and emerging SOP Lang circuits. The book treats negative results and unresolved questions as part of the evidence, rather than presenting a finished general intelligence. It invites researchers and builders to inspect the mechanisms, reproduce bounded experiments and identify the next questions worth testing.
