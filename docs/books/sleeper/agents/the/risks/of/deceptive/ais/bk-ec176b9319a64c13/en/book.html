Sleeper Agents: The Risks of Deceptive AIs

Hidden triggers, model safety and public accountability

A model can appear safe while concealing a triggered behavior. The book connects sleeper agents with persistent cyber threats and poisoned training data. It explains why ordinary safety training may leave hidden backdoors intact. Twenty speculative attack scenarios illustrate possible social and infrastructure risks. Later chapters examine transparency, open models and community-controlled AI centers. The conclusion calls for audits, shared standards and public accountability.

About Book

This book begins with research on deceptive behavior that can persist in language models after safety training. It compares hidden model triggers with persistent cyber threats, then asks how poisoning, evaluation blind spots and synthetic data could spread or conceal risk.

Its twenty-row scenario table is explicitly speculative, ranging from misinformation and market manipulation to infrastructure disruption. Subsequent chapters consider interpretability, open models, community governance, state security measures and public-private cooperation. The book argues for continuing audits and responsibility across institutions.
