Symbolic World Models

How an AI system preserves the meaning of a situation

How can an AI assistant follow a situation as a conversation changes? This book examines symbolic world models and neurosymbolic reasoning. It explores world patterns, particular instances, and the interpretation of language. Travel, organizations, stories, programs, and connected buildings provide its examples. Questions of uncertainty, revision, and external action run throughout the discussion. It addresses readers interested in the architecture of systems that track changing context.

About Book

A changed departure date can affect a meeting, a reservation, and the heating of a home. This book uses such situations to investigate what an AI system must represent when language, reasoning, and action concern the same evolving circumstances. Its central questions involve the continuity of meaning and the distinctions that different activities require.

The discussion introduces symbolic world models through world patterns and particular instances. It follows threads, circuits, interpretation, matching, validation, and execution across travel planning, organizations, narrative perspectives, programs, and connected buildings. The proposed architecture raises questions about evidence, uncertainty, revision, and the relationship between a calculated possibility and an observed result.
