Albert Einstein: The Man Behind the Equations

Relativity, quantum physics and the life of a public thinker

Einstein’s thought experiments changed how physicists understood time, space and gravity. His 1905 work connects special relativity, Brownian motion and the photoelectric effect. General relativity links curved spacetime with observations from eclipses to gravitational waves. The book follows his education, scientific collaborations and search for a unified field theory. It also examines pacifism, civil rights, nuclear responsibility and his philosophical disagreements with quantum mechanics. A closing account traces his influence on technology, culture and modern research.
