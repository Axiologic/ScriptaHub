To Swipe or Not to Swipe: Hamlet’s Dilemma

A satirical digital remix of Shakespeare’s tragedy

This satirical essay asks whether Hamlet still speaks to a digital generation. Its first chapter turns indecision and soliloquies into social feeds and streaming choices. The second recasts Elsinore as a workplace of personal brands and public relations. Hamletonomics imagines the absurd industries created by revenge and court intrigue. About Book

This satirical essay asks whether Hamlet still speaks to a digital generation. Its first chapter turns indecision and soliloquies into social feeds and streaming choices. The second recasts Elsinore as a workplace of personal brands and public relations.

Hamletonomics imagines the absurd industries created by revenge and court intrigue. The digital remake offers apps, polls, viral challenges, and a quieter therapeutic alternative. The conclusion uses humor as a bridge back to Shakespeare’s original play. The edition keeps the four substantive chapters and conclusion while leaving behind the old promotional preface and annex.
