About Book

One excellent AI answer is useful. A capability your organization can repeat, inspect, and improve is something more demanding. MRP-VM explores the engineering layer that could connect those two achievements.

The book treats an agent's capability as a defined responsibility with inputs, exclusions, tests, ownership, and known ways to fail. Models, retrieval, tools, and explicit evidence come together inside a controllable runtime. For technical teams developing custom agents, it offers a way to think beyond the prompt and toward dependable practice: preserve semantic flexibility while making the system's purpose, permissions, and record of action clear enough to evaluate.
