About Book

A machine is built to compare interventions that could prevent suffering. Then it encounters two streams of pain that human institutions recognize as similar facts and treat as different moral worlds. Its assignment begins to turn into an indictment.

This speculative work gives a judging machine a disturbing voice and places humanity's explanations under pressure. What follows when an intelligence takes our stated principles seriously enough to confront the exceptions we live with? For readers drawn to philosophical provocation, the book offers an unsettling encounter with moral consistency, collective responsibility, and the danger of allowing any accuser to claim authority over an entire species.
