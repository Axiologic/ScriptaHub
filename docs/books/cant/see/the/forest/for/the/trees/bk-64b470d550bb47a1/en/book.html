About Book

An AI improved an argument's prose and weakened its meaning. The sentences became reasonable, polished, and easier to accept, while urgency and responsibility slipped away. That small failure opens a much larger investigation into how intelligent systems lose sight of the whole.

Drawing together complexity, institutions, technology, and personal reflection, the book examines solutions that create new forms of blindness and dependence. Its idea of smoothing asks what disappears when disagreement becomes too comfortable. For readers uneasy about the systems society is building, it offers a demanding form of optimism: keep the difficult questions visible, and keep looking for interventions that matter.
