About Book

Organized in ten thematic chapters, this book asks readers to reconsider familiar explanations for social life. Its questions cover traditions, democratic power, love, marriage, poverty, work, religion, identity, justice, consumer culture, and digital technology. Many entries pair a conventional answer with a more suspicious interpretation of the same practice.

Those interpretations are presented as possibilities to examine, not as verified proof of a hidden plan. The collection is strongest as an invitation to compare claims, evidence, and incentives across different parts of daily life. The conclusion returns to free will and the influence of institutions and stories on personal choice. This edition retains the question chapters and substantive conclusion while removing the former project’s promotional preface and annexes.
