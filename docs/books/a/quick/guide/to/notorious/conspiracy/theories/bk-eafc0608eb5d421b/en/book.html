About Book

A Quick Guide to Notorious Conspiracy Theories moves through hidden government power, technological fears, extraterrestrial stories, and cultural or religious allegations. It distinguishes documented events, such as MKUltra, from unsupported extensions of those events. Its discussions of HAARP, 5G, the Moon landings, Area 51, and shared false memories compare vivid claims with scientific or historical counterarguments.

The book also addresses harmful claims about vaccines, Pizzagate, and the forged Protocols of the Elders of Zion. Its recurring question is what evidence could verify or disprove a theory. The original assigns precise likelihoods without explaining a reproducible method; these figures remain source claims rather than established probabilities.
