A Report on Secret Societies and Real Conspiracies

Covert action, institutional power and the evidence behind contested claims

The report asks how documented covert action can be distinguished from speculation. It examines corporate incentives around opioids, private prisons and lobbying. Forced sterilization, biological testing, MKUltra and Project Sunshine illustrate abuses of state and medical power. Accounts of the USS Liberty and Gulf of Tonkin require attention to disputed evidence and official explanations. Cambridge Analytica, election interference and online quizzes connect political influence with personal data. The Fellowship, Freemasonry, Illuminati, Opus Dei and Skull and Bones are considered through membership, influence and myth.
