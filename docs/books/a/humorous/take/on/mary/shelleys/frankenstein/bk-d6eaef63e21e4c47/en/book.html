About Book

This brisk retelling follows Mary Shelley’s Frankenstein through Victor’s experiment, the creature’s lonely education, and the escalating conflict between them. Its narrator uses television jokes, deliberately exaggerated comparisons, and direct addresses to the reader. The comic voice makes the story accessible while returning to the harm caused by abandonment and silence.

The four chapters move from Victor’s ambition to the creature’s experience of rejection, then to revenge and a reflection on the novel’s themes. The final section considers scientific responsibility, belonging, artificial intelligence, and genetic engineering as questions raised by the fiction. Readers can enjoy the humor and still examine where sympathy, blame, and care belong.
