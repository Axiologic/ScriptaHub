About Book

What does humanity lose when it finally learns to prevent catastrophe? In Port Nacre, a system called the Measure makes the costs of water, energy, and consumption visible. Life is safer, but the machinery of survival has begun to shape the meaning of freedom.

Then an elderly architect of this order receives a synthetic witness speaking in his younger voice. It was created to expose his compromises, and its arrival signals a crisis. This speculative novel brings identity, ecological scarcity, and political responsibility into an intimate confrontation. Enter a future where saving civilization may require recovering its capacity to disagree.
