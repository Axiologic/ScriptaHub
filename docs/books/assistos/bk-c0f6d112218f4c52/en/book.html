About Book

What if you could give an AI worker a useful assignment, leave it working and return to a result you can actually inspect? The second edition of AssistOS begins with that everyday experience. Follow a project from its first task through documents, code, research and browser work, with people keeping control of decisions and accepted changes.

The book introduces persistent Robots, the shared Explorer workspace, WebChat, WebMeet and Ploinky’s installable capabilities. It shows why permissions, reusable procedures, private workspaces and recovery matter as much as model intelligence. For builders, teams and curious readers, it offers a practical route into the proposed platform while clearly separating available building blocks from development and research ambitions.
