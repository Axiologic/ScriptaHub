Affective Calculus

A Cognitive Grammar of Human Emotions, Feelings and Attitudes for Verifiable AI

This conceptual monograph proposes Appraisal Affective Calculus as a testable model of human affect. It separates emotions, moods, relational feelings, attitudes and action policies. An appraisal vector connects goals, control, norms, dignity and attachment to affective interpretations. Its atlas distinguishes fear from anger, guilt from shame and respect from admiration. Literary subtext and counterfactual probes test whether AI explains more than emotion words. The book distinguishes functional simulation from experience and examines risks of manipulation.
