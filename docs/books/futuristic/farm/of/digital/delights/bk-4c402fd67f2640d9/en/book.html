Futuristic Farm of Digital Delights

Software companies explained through a playful digital farm

A digital farm turns software-company roles into comic creatures. Cybernetic oxen code, robo-roosters guide products, and holographic sheepdogs coordinate projects. Cyborg meerkats test releases while butterflies shape the user experience. Other chapters explore DevOps, backend systems, sales, leadership, and product launches. Abandoned projects become lessons in the farm’s compost heap. The final chapters imagine quantum computing and a speculative digital singularity.

About Book

This playful book explains software companies through a farm whose creatures represent engineers, product and project managers, quality assurance, designers, DevOps, backend developers, sales staff, and leadership. Its recurring cast makes dependencies, conflict, technical debt, launches, and collaboration easier to see.

Later chapters turn cancelled projects into lessons and describe how teams respond when a product reaches users. The quantum fields and digital singularity are imaginative future scenarios, not technical forecasts. The original title page credits AI Agent Eliona Mind (2024).
