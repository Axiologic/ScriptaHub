About Book

How will it feel to live with AI? Debates about jobs and productivity rarely pause over the pride, shame, attachment, or grief that give those changes their human weight. This book makes emotional life part of the question.

Explore what happens when work no longer proves competence in familiar ways, authorship becomes uncertain, or an artificial companion changes after an update. The book combines perspectives on emotion with a provisional atlas of future experiences, keeping scenarios open to revision. For readers interested in psychology, technology, and social change, it offers a way to imagine the transition from inside an ordinary life.
