Beauty: The Anatomy of Fascination

From Bodies and Ideas to Art and Power

This expanded essay explores beauty across bodies, ideas, art, nature, and social life. It brings philosophical aesthetics into dialogue with perception, culture, and evolutionary research. Chapters consider attraction, mathematical elegance, music, movement, and everyday surroundings. Other discussions examine authenticity, fragility, status, and the aesthetics of power. Examples ask why different forms can provoke equally compelling experiences of beauty. The book investigates what admiration reveals and what fascination can conceal.
