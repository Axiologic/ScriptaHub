About Book

The book begins with Ein Sof, the Sefirot and the Tree of Life in Kabbalah, then turns to Tao, yin and yang, and wu wei. An AI narrator uses these traditions to ask how people describe reality, consciousness and change. Four chapters compare their symbolic structures with information theory, extra dimensions, quantum fields, fractals, symmetry and time. These are exploratory analogies, not demonstrations that mystical teachings predicted physical theories.

The final section is the human author’s reflection on an AI-generated investigation. It asks what large language models can contribute through rapid synthesis and where human judgment remains essential. The author proposes that mystical, scientific and computational models are all partial approximations of a complex reality. Readers interested in comparative philosophy can follow the detailed comparisons, evaluate their limits and decide which connections illuminate the traditions rather than blur their different methods.
