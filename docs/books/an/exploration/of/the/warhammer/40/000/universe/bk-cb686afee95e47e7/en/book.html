An Exploration of the Warhammer 40,000 Universe

Factions, institutions and warfare in the 41st millennium

The Imperium fights for survival while its harsh institutions consume the people they claim to protect. Space Marines, the Imperial Guard and the Adeptus Mechanicus embody distinct forms of military and technological power. Chaos, Orks, Eldar, Tyranids, Necrons and T'au bring competing threats and ambitions to the galaxy. The Inquisition, Arbites and Administratum expose the scale and brutality of Imperial government. Warp travel, ancient machines, psychic powers and living weapons shape conflicts across worlds. The book explores how faith, scarcity and unending war structure life in this fictional universe.
