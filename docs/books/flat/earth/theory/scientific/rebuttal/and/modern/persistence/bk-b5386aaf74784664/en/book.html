About Book

The book begins with NASA and the conspiracy claims made about its images, astronauts and satellites. It then traces ancient ideas about Earth’s shape and the modern movement from Samuel Rowbotham to internet communities. These sections distinguish the history of a belief from the evidence needed to support it.

Later chapters examine falsifiability, selective use of evidence and the social appeal of hidden knowledge. The book tests flat Earth claims against circumnavigation, gravity, eclipses, changing constellations and the Foucault pendulum. It closes by considering how scientists and educators can answer misinformation while strengthening critical thinking.
