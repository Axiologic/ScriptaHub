Between Faith and Evidence

Science, Religion, and the Temptation of Certainty

This agnostic investigation examines science, religion, and competing claims to certainty. Twenty-five historical dossiers explore beliefs associated with influential thinkers. The discussion separates biographical testimony from scientific and philosophical arguments. Chapters clarify materialism, atheism, agnosticism, and the boundaries of expert authority. Educational questions connect intellectual history with evidence and responsibility. The book asks how uncertainty can coexist with meaningful differences between claims.
