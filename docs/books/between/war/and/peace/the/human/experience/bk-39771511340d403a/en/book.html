Between War and Peace: The Human Experience

Pierre, Andrei, and Natasha under the pressure of war

This thematic reading follows three characters from Tolstoy’s War and Peace. Pierre seeks genuine connection beyond the rituals of Russian high society. Andrei weighs military duty against loss, disillusionment, and love for Natasha. Natasha’s hopes change as war unsettles her family and relationships. Ballrooms and battlefields reveal the human cost beneath ideals of glory. The conclusion treats peace as a continuing choice shaped by dialogue and compassion.
