About Book

This book examines Isaac Asimov’s Foundation series through the fall of the Galactic Empire and Hari Seldon’s attempt to shorten the dark age that follows. Its opening chapters explain psychohistory, the Seldon Plan and the tension between predictable collective patterns and unexpected individual decisions. The Mule makes that tension impossible to ignore.

Later chapters follow political maneuvering, the Second Foundation and the changing sources of the Foundation’s influence. Knowledge survives through archives, learning, innovation and adaptation, yet its control also grants power. The book compares imperial decline with historical empires and closes with key events from the original novels, asking what freedom remains when leaders claim to know history’s course.
