Before Explanation

Toward an Outfinitist Science of Limits

This book examines how explanations shape understanding, responsibility, and power in everyday life. It follows the distance between persuasive stories and the realities they claim to describe. Its chapters connect cognitive psychology, institutional decisions, personal relationships, and artificial intelligence. Readers encounter questions about evidence, uncertainty, competing interpretations, and the boundaries of useful models. The final part introduces outfinitism as a proposed research programme concerned with limits of knowledge. The discussion addresses readers interested in reasoning, organizational judgment, and the ethical stakes of making sense of experience.
