World as a System: 1000 Reasoning Problems for Grades 1–4

1000 Reasoning Problems in Geography, History, Civics, and Environmental Studies

This workbook offers one thousand reasoning problems for children in grades one through four. Problems use explicit facts, rules, relationships, and constraints rather than hidden knowledge. Geography exercises cover routes, rivers, maps, climate, and regions. History tasks explore evidence, chronology, change, and conflicting sources. Civics and environmental studies introduce decisions, rights, systems, and consequences. The sequence develops reasoning skills across fifty families of problems.
