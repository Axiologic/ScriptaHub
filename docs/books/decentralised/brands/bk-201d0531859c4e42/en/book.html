About Book

A brand is a promise that can travel farther than the person who made it. But when employees, customers, and communities build its meaning together, who should control the name and the value it carries?

This book explores brands as institutions, connecting the history of shared symbols with proposals for decentralized ownership and governance. It asks how communities can protect quality, reward contributions, and resist capture while preserving the freedom to leave. For builders, organizers, and readers interested in the future of business, it opens a conversation about names that people can belong to, help govern, and sustain together.
