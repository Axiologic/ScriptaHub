About Book

An exact solver can calculate the wrong answer perfectly if the original question was translated incorrectly. As AI connects everyday language to executable systems, that gap becomes one of the most consequential places for errors to hide.

This book introduces a proposed controlled language and a visible path from source statements to interpretations, assumptions, and execution. It examines how identities, quantifiers, and evidence can retain their meaning as prose becomes computation. For researchers and developers working with language, reasoning, or scientific software, it offers an architectural challenge: make the interpretation inspectable before the precision of the result makes it look unquestionable.
