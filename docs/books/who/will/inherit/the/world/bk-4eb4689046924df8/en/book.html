Who Will Inherit the World?

Flaws, Fertility, and the Unseen Selection of Civilizations

This scientific and philosophical essay examines how social environments shape reproductive differences. It connects fertility, personality, inherited variation, and cultural transmission. Historical chapters explore marriage rules, migration, religion, cities, and institutions. The discussion separates evidence from hypotheses about evolutionary change. Seven future scenarios consider AI, community continuity, and reproductive autonomy. Its central question concerns the tension between what persists and what deserves to persist.
