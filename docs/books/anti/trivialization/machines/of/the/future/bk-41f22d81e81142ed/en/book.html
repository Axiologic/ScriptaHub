About Book

Why do activities that need patience keep being reorganized around clicks, reactions, and speed? This book follows the pressure that turns research, education, politics, and work into contests for immediate attention.

Its proposed anti-trivialization machines are institutions and practices that protect context, memory, and time for judgment. Archives, professional duties, and decision procedures become tools for preserving what rapid feedback overlooks. As AI makes plausible answers abundant, the question grows more urgent: who will do the slower work of evaluation? Readers interested in education, media, and institutional design will find an invitation to build environments where serious thought has room to survive.
