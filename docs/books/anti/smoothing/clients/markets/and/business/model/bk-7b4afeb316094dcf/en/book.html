Anti-Smoothing: Clients, Markets, and Business Model

A business thesis on evidence, decisions, and the cost of lost nuance.

Anti-Smoothing proposes checking what disappears between evidence and a business decision. Its first proposed customers are AI software suppliers and research agencies. The manuscript compares bounded reviews, subscriptions, and later software integration. It defines human review, source access, objections, and confidentiality limits. Prices and market estimates are hypotheses for paid experiments. Its launch plan tests repeat demand before broader expansion.

About Book

The book develops a commercial hypothesis for reviewing whether summaries, promises, or reports omit conditions that matter to a decision. It compares a focused startup, an integrable service, and a possible shared operator brand.

Fifteen chapters examine buyers, pilot-to-contract reviews, research reports, competing tools, packages, market sizing, delivery costs, security, and a ninety-day launch plan. Two appendices provide a sample decision dossier and an initial commercial offer. Prices, conversion assumptions, and projections are presented as proposals to test rather than established market results.
