We Manufacture Our Own Masters

Power, Freedom, Resentment, and Artificial Intelligence

This book asks how people repeatedly build systems that come to dominate them. It examines equality, capitalism, speech, expertise, resentment, and organized force. Political ideals are tested against the mechanisms that may reverse them. Artificial intelligence could reduce scarcity while creating new forms of dependence. The chapters distinguish local freedom from control over large institutions. The argument seeks arrangements that expose contradictions and permit correction.
