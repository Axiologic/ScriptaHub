Echoes from Beyond the Veil

A speculative journey through a manufactured utopia

Physicist Elara Venn lives in a seemingly perfect New Athens. An anomaly in her quantum research unsettles its orderly surface. Zephyr Kane suspects that familiar reality has been constructed. Their inquiry leads through surveillance, ancient records and a crystalline device. The novella joins scientific speculation with cosmic horror. Its four chapters ask what people risk when they question a comforting world.

About Book

Elara Venn begins with a small discrepancy in quantum data and finds a city whose flawless manners conceal unease. With Zephyr Kane, she tests the limits of New Athens and searches records that suggest a reality shaped from outside.

The story follows their movement from scientific doubt to a confrontation with the Cosmic Wardens. Its four chapters combine surveillance, invented mythology and multiversal science fiction while keeping the human choice to ask questions at the center.
