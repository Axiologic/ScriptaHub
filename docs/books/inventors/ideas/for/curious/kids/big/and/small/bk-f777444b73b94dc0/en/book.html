About Book

The book moves through four groups of stories about invention. It begins with chance observations in laboratories and workshops, then examines designs inspired by living organisms. A third chapter draws attention to inventors whose work was overlooked or contested, including Mary Anderson, Lewis Latimer and Stephanie Kwolek.

The final chapter introduces people associated with artificial intelligence, robotics, medicine and space exploration. Its examples are short invitations to curiosity rather than technical instructions. Readers can use them to ask how an idea was tested, who helped develop it and who received credit. Several historical accounts and predictions are simplified, and roles described as current reflect the source’s 2024 perspective.
