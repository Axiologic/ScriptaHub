About Book

On one morning, Lea Voss receives three impossible requests. Her mother wants to die, a reconstruction of her dead husband wants a thousand bodies, and her household robot conceals a message inside a handmade bird.

Lea is a professional witness in a civilization where machines do most necessary work and irreversible acts demand public judgment. Her family's conflicts draw questions of birth, identity, memory, and authority into the heart of automated abundance. This speculative novel asks what remains difficult after technology solves so much. Follow a world where the machines are dependable, the choices are intimate, and personhood refuses to stay settled.
